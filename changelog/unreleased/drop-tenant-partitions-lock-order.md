### Fixed

- **`nexus.drop_tenant_partitions` no longer deadlocks with a search.** It detached and dropped a tenant's leaves one at a time, holding each ACCESS EXCLUSIVE lock to commit, while a search enters through the root `nexus.chunks` and locks partitions after it, so the two could wait on each other (engine 0.1.199, 2026-11-20: `deadlock detected`, the drop's `DETACH PARTITION` the victim). The function now takes `nexus.chunks` and `nexus.taxonomy_centroids` ACCESS EXCLUSIVE first, in one statement, after its deletes and before any detach (changeset `vectors-063-1`, a function redefinition: no data effect, no wire change). The wait is bounded so searches cannot pile up behind it: five attempts of 500 ms with a 0.2 s x attempt backoff, then `lock_not_available` naming the tenant ("could not take the root locks without stalling searches"); the call is one transaction, so a failed call changes nothing and a rerun finishes the drop. The roots stay locked from the first detach to commit, and the function raises a NOTICE with the attempt taken and the hold time in milliseconds.
