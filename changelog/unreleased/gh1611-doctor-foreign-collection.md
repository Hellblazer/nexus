### Added

- **`nx doctor` names documents whose manifest chunks are not in their own collection** (GH #1611). A repo index that resolved a file onto another owner's document (fixed above) left that document with `physical_collection` set to the curator's collection while its manifest named chunks in the repository's collection: a search scoped to the curator's collection missed them and its live count fell short of its stored count. The new row reads the engine's new bounded route `GET /v1/catalog/manifest/foreign_collection?limit=N` (one statement, tenant-scoped, a statement timeout answers 503) and warns with up to ten documents, the other collections each names, and the total. An engine without the route, or a read that times out, is "cannot say", never "none": informational while the engine floor is at or below 0.1.188, a warning after. The fix text is honest about the repair: `nx index pdf <file> --collection <its collection> --force` replaces all of the document's manifest rows with rows naming that collection but does not remove the copy in the repository's collection, which the engine moves to quarantine only after 30 days with no owner (`nx t3 gc -c <collection>`).
