### Changed

- **`nx enrich bib` looks up paper documents only, by default** (GH #1611). It looked up every titled catalog document of the collection, knowledge notes and markdown included, and since a miss writes nothing, each of those was looked up again on every run. Documents of another content type are now counted in the run's summary and left alone; `--content-type any` restores the old reach, and `--content-type <type>` (`paper`, `pdf`, `markdown`, `prose`, `code`, `rdr`, `knowledge`) picks one type; any other value is refused. `nx dt index --enrich` and `nx dt capture --enrich` keep looking up every type.
