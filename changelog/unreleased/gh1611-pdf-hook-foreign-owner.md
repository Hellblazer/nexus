### Fixed

- **`nx index pdf` no longer stamps its collection, title or metadata onto another owner's document** (GH #1611). When a repository run had already registered the PDF, `nx index pdf <file> --collection ...` found that row by its source URI (the lookup is not owner-scoped) and wrote its own `physical_collection`, title, author, year and extraction record onto it, pointing the repository's document at a collection holding chunks it does not own. The catalog step now leaves alone a row of another owner that holds a different collection and logs `catalog_pdf_hook_skipped_foreign_owner_row` (with the collection the row holds); a row of the PDF's own owner, or another owner's row already in the run's collection, is updated as before. This direction is not fully fixed: the run's pre-flight still writes the PDF's chunks, and the document's manifest, under the other owner's tumbler in the run's collection, so the row is left split (its `physical_collection` names the repository's collection while its manifest names the run's). `nx doctor` now names such a row ("manifest chunks outside the document's collection") with the steps that repair it.
