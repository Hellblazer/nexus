### Changed

- **`memory_search` asks the engine for its page instead of fetching every match (RDR-209 Phase 0b).** The MCP tool sends its `limit` and `offset` to `POST /v1/memory/search`, so only the rows on the page are read and have their `access_count` and `last_accessed` bumped; before, every match was fetched and stamped and the page was cut in the client. The output is unchanged (`showing a-b of T`, `next: offset=...`, `(end)`, `No results at offset N (total T).`). A `limit` below 1 is now a usage error before any request. Against an engine older than 0.1.196, which ignores the page, the client cuts the page itself and still reports the total, but that engine has already stamped every match. `nx memory search` and the client's `search()` are unchanged: they print and stamp every match. New client method: `HttpMemoryStore.search_page` / `T2Database.search_page`, returning `(rows, matching_total)`.
