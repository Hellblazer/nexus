### Added

- **`nx memory gate-rollup` keeps a closed phase's gate records in full in T3 and lists them in a pointer record.** `nx memory gate-rollup --rdr N (--phase P --beads b1,b2,... | --all) [--project REPO] [--dry-run]` reads the repository's T2 project once and matches whole tags: a record with `kind:gate` belongs to a listed bead by `bead:<id>`, or with `--all` to the RDR by `rdr:<NNN>`, and with `--phase` an older untagged record belongs to a bead its title names as a whole token (`nexus-1` is not found in `nexus-12`). Each listed record's full text is written to the knowledge subject `nexus-rdr-research` as a note of its own (category `gate_record`, titled `<project>/<T2 title>`, split into titled parts when over the 16,384-byte store_put cap) and read back before anything else is written. One pointer note, `gate-records-rdr-<NNN>-phase-<P>` (or `-all`), category `gate_pointer`, lists each record's title, kind, bead, reviewed sha, verdict, timestamp and archive; a rerun replaces it, and one over the cap is written in titled parts. A `kind:gate` record of the RDR with no `bead:` tag (under `--phase`, one whose title names the phase) and a record with no readable verdict are listed as unmapped and are not archived. Before it writes anything it refuses an open phase and a bead whose newest record is not justified (unless `--accept-open <bead>`), and a record an earlier pointer listed that the reap since deleted stays in the new pointer. Then it gives each listed record that has no TTL `ttl=30` through the verbatim import route (content, tags, agent, session, `access_count`, `last_accessed` and timestamp stay as they were; the route also clears the quarantine stamps and re-stamps the row's identity) and reads the rows back; `--dry-run` reports how many the engine's expiry rule would quarantine at the next sweep and records that count in T2; a real run that would write a TTL refuses unless a dry run of the same RDR and phase recorded its count and `--counted N` matches it, and a record a person restored is not given a TTL again (RDR-209 Phase 3). The `phase-review-gate` and `rdr-close` skills name the command.
