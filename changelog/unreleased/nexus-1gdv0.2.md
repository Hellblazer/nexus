### Added

- **`nx memory gate-rollup` keeps a closed phase's gate records in full in T3 and lists them in a pointer record.** `nx memory gate-rollup --rdr N (--phase P --beads b1,b2,... | --all) [--project REPO] [--dry-run]` reads the repository's T2 project once and matches whole tags: a record with `kind:gate` belongs to a listed bead by `bead:<id>`, or with `--all` to the RDR by `rdr:<NNN>`, and with `--phase` an older untagged record belongs to a bead its title names as a whole token (`nexus-1` is not found in `nexus-12`). Each listed record's full text is written to the knowledge subject `nexus-rdr-research` as a note of its own (category `gate_record`, titled `<project>/<T2 title>`, split into titled parts when over the 16,384-byte store_put cap) and read back before anything else is written. One pointer note, `gate-records-rdr-<NNN>-phase-<P>` (or `-all`), category `gate_pointer`, lists each record's title, kind, bead, reviewed sha, verdict, timestamp and archive; a rerun replaces it, and one over the cap is written in titled parts. A `kind:gate` record of the RDR with no `bead:` tag (under `--phase`, one whose title names the phase) and a record with no readable verdict are listed as unmapped and are not archived. It writes nothing to T2: no TTL, no tag, no change (RDR-209 Phase 3 Step 1; the loss check and the 30-day TTL are Steps 2 and 3). The `phase-review-gate` and `rdr-close` skills name the command.
