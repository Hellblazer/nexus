### Changed

- **Four test-apparatus checks report instead of failing develop (RDR-258 Phase 1).** The storage-touch ratchet fails only above `alarm_line_tests = 5544` in `tests/storage_touch_ceiling.toml`; between its baseline 5394 and the line a run prints `storage_touch value=... delta=... trend(10)=...` to the job summary with a warning and passes, so a change that mints a few tenants no longer edits the ceiling file. `docs_reference_rot`'s six repo-state tests, `module_seam`'s two repo-state scans and the mode-declarations census's lint twin carry a `class_a` marker (always beside `lint`) and run in the advisory `test-class-a-report` job, which `test-lint` (`lint and not class_a`) and `pytest-gate` do not wait on, and in the nightly `slow` job; the census itself keeps its own job and runs through the same reporter. `scripts/class_a_report.py` lists each failing item as an annotation, a `CLASS_A_ITEM <check> <id>` line, a row of an items file the job uploads for 30 days, and the job summary, and exits 0; it fails only when it examined too little, per check (`--require`). The docs-readers selector no longer names `tests/test_docs_reference_rot.py`. Locally, the pre-push meta-test sweep skips `class_a`, and `NX_CLASS_A_MODE=report` turns a census offender into a warning in the sweep's guard run and the preflight changed-tests step.
- **The red-run report counts job timeouts and cancels.** `scripts/red_run_stuck.py` records, at extraction, runs that ended `timed_out` or `cancelled` and failed runs that carry a job that hit its timeout (a cancelled job inside a failed run is how a hung test looks), and the report lists the ones whose job hit it. The red-run count itself is unchanged.
