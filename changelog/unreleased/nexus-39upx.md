### Fixed

- **The superseded-chunk sweep warning says what a skipped sweep leaves.** A dropped chunk no other document owns is out of search and the get/list reads (they require a live owner, RDR-192), stays in T3, and is still returned by default by a lookup by chash until the engine reaper normally collects it: quarantined after a 30-day grace, deleted 14 days later. The warning used to say the rows may still be searchable and to run `nx t3 gc -c COLLECTION`, and the fail-loud exception repeated that advice; `nx t3 gc` uses the reaper's grace and reclaims nothing sooner, so the warning and the deferred-sweep line (nexus-4pj54) no longer offer it, and the exception names it only for an install whose reaper is disabled or refuses, once the grace has passed. The warning counts distinct documents, and each summary with skips logs one `superseded_sweep_skipped` event with the skip count, the document count, the count per reason and the collections. The skip still counts toward the non-zero exit.
