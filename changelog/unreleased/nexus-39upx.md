### Fixed

- **The superseded-chunk sweep warning says what a skipped sweep leaves.** A dropped chunk no other document owns is out of search and the get/list reads (they require a live owner, RDR-192), stays in T3, and is still returned by a lookup by chash until the engine reaper collects it: quarantined after a 30-day grace, deleted 14 days later. The warning used to say the rows may still be searchable and to run `nx t3 gc -c COLLECTION`, and the fail-loud exception repeated that advice; `nx t3 gc` uses the reaper's grace and reclaimed nothing sooner, so the advice is gone from both, and from the deferred-sweep line (nexus-4pj54). The warning counts distinct documents, and each summary with skips logs one `superseded_sweep_skipped` event with the skip count, the document count, the count per reason and the collections. The skip still counts toward the non-zero exit.
