### Removed

- **The `nexus.frecency` table, its two telemetry routes and the client's `import_frecency_row` are gone** (nexus-6pfgb.42; engine and client; rides the next engine tag). Nothing wrote or read the table: its one client writer was an ETL import whose last caller was deleted, and `POST /v1/telemetry/frecency/upsert` and `GET /v1/telemetry/frecency/get` were called only by tests. The score search reads is the `frecency_score` key on a chunk's own metadata (written through `/v1/vectors/update-metadata`), and that is unchanged. The `nexus_diag` counts view `nexus.diag_chash_conformance` loses its frecency leg (the runAlways `taxonomy-011-8` re-creates it with four legs on every boot), and changeset `telemetry-019-1` then drops the table and its rows; its rollback recreates the table empty. The `frecency` kind of `POST /v1/telemetry/import`, `/import_batch` and `/ids/probe` now answers 400 `Unknown table`, `nexus.frecency` is no longer a count-only relation, `nexus.db.chash_tables.CHASH_BEARING_TABLES` and `nx doctor`'s RLS list lose it, and a tenant backup written before this change (`nx backup`, RDR-248) still restores: its `frecency` section is skipped and named in the import status notes, never refused. The drop runs automatically at the first boot of the engine that carries it, and no copy of the rows is kept (nothing in the engine reads them). A view another role owns that still reads the table stops the engine from starting until that role re-creates the view without the frecency leg or drops it; a view the migrating role owns is re-pointed by the same boot. **Engine rollback.** Once `telemetry-019-1` has run, an older engine cannot boot against that database: its runAlways `taxonomy-011-8` re-creates the five-leg view and fails on the missing table. Rolling the engine back therefore needs `telemetry-019-1` rolled back first (it recreates the empty table), using this engine's changelog.
