### Added

- **`nx upgrade` now runs the retired-key sweep on a local install: a new ladder rung, `retire-keys-sweep`.** It strips the chunk-row metadata keys RDR-236 retired (`title`, `tags`, the section labels, the spans, ...) from the install's tenant, through the engine's own sweep job (the one `nx t3 retire-keys` drives), after the manifest backfill, section-label, span-fill and note-tags rungs. A local install presents the root credential the route accepts, and `nx upgrade --auto` runs the sweep too: that is a real unattended write at session start until the install is swept. It never accepts a residue for you: when a precondition stands in the way (legacy-label rows, keyless notes, home-less documents, NULL spans, generated NULLs) the rung defers and writes nothing. The deferral names each count and what accepting it destroys (the sweep keeps no copy of a removed key; `tags=` and `category=` filters stop finding the documents of an accepted `generated_nulls`), and offers `--accept NAME=N` only for a residue the engine lets you accept. A credential the route refuses (a managed install's mint or data credential), a sweep job another request is running, and a busy engine defer the rung and name `nx t3 retire-keys`; against a managed service `--auto` runs no dry run and no sweep. It is a data move at `nx upgrade`, never at engine boot. Rows the sweep cannot take (a wrong-width chash, a blocked collection) defer the confirmation. Once it is recorded the RDR-192 backfill rung stops running, and `nx doctor` gains a `Retired chunk keys` row that counts the rows as of the last `nx upgrade` measurement. The add-constraint and validate steps wait for the Step 2c engine cut (nexus-6pfgb.43, nexus-6pfgb.66).
