### Added

- **`nx upgrade` now runs the retired-key sweep on every install: a new ladder rung, `retire-keys-sweep`.** It strips the chunk-row metadata keys RDR-236 retired (`title`, `tags`, the section labels, the spans, ...) from this install's tenant, through the engine's own sweep job (the one `nx t3 retire-keys` drives), after the manifest backfill, section-label, span-fill and note-tags rungs. It never accepts a residue for you: when a precondition stands in the way (legacy-label rows, keyless notes, home-less documents, NULL spans, generated NULLs) the rung defers, writes nothing, and names each count with the `nx t3 retire-keys --tenant T --apply --accept NAME=N` command. It is a data move at `nx upgrade`, never at engine boot. Unattended (`--auto`) against a managed service it runs no dry run and no sweep (that sweep is an operator verb). The add-constraint and validate steps wait for the Step 2c engine cut (nexus-6pfgb.43, nexus-6pfgb.66).
