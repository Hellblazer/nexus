### Added

- **The engine now logs and counts client chunk writes that name a metadata key a chunk row will not keep, and names who sent them.** On `upsert-chunks`, `update-metadata` and the `write_many`, `append` and `append_many` writes that carry chunks, a request whose chunk metadata names any key other than `chunk_text_hash` and `embedding_model` gets a `chunk_metadata_key_would_refuse` log line. The line carries the route, tenant and collection, the offending keys (the unknown `other` keys first and in full, then the known classes `document`, `position` and `legacy`, each listed or collapsed to `class:+N`), the counts of offending and requested chunks, the client's `User-Agent` and `X-Nexus-Client-Version`, and the `source_agent` of the first offending chunk. It is limited to one line per route, tenant, collection, key set and client per minute, so a second writer in the same collection logs its own line. `GET /v1/status` gains `chunk_metadata_key_would_refuse_total` (one per request, never rate limited) and three bounded views of the same count: `chunk_metadata_key_would_refuse_by_route_class` (`route`, `key_class`, `count`), `chunk_metadata_key_would_refuse_by_key` (the 32 most frequent keys as `key`, `key_class`, `count`, plus `overflow`) and `chunk_metadata_key_would_refuse_by_client` (`user_agent`, `client_version`, `count` for up to 64 clients, plus `overflow`). The count is of requests that offered a key, taken before the write: it includes requests a later step refuses and chunks the append routes later drop, and does not see the engine's own writers (re-embed, gc restore) or pre-upgrade staged PDF rows until they are replayed. The write proceeds unchanged: this is the log-mode window before the engine refuses such writes (RDR-236 Phase 4 Step 2). `taxonomy__` and `quarantine-` collections are skipped (nexus-6pfgb.66).
