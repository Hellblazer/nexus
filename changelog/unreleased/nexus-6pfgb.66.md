### Added

- **The engine now logs and counts client chunk writes that name a metadata key a chunk row will not keep.** On `upsert-chunks`, `update-metadata` and the `write_many`, `append` and `append_many` writes that carry chunks, a request whose chunk metadata names any key other than `chunk_text_hash` and `embedding_model` gets one `chunk_metadata_key_would_refuse` log line per route, tenant and collection per minute (the offending keys by class, document, position, legacy or other; the client's `User-Agent` and `X-Nexus-Client-Version`), and `GET /v1/status` gains `chunk_metadata_key_would_refuse_total`, counted once per request and never rate limited. The write proceeds unchanged: this is the log-mode window before the engine refuses such writes (RDR-236 Phase 4 Step 2). `taxonomy__` and `quarantine-` collections are skipped (nexus-6pfgb.66).
