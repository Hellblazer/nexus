### Added

- **The engine counts a collection's topic coverage and caches it (RDR-134 Step 2).** New functions `nexus.taxonomy_coverage_counts_384`, `_768` and `_1024` (`taxonomy-022`) return one row of (has_taxonomy, chunks, unassigned) over the collection's live chunks, executable by `nexus_svc` only. `taxonomy-023` counts them in one set-based aggregation, so a collection whose manifest has no planner statistics yet (a fresh install, a restore, a tenant import) is counted in a fraction of a second instead of running past the 30 s bound. The engine caches it per tenant and collection. Every store, assignment, topic, centroid and catalog manifest write that reaches the engine's routes drops the cache, and the reconcile-commit and manifest-fill jobs drop the tenant when they end; writers outside those routes are covered by a ten-minute ceiling. Nothing reads the cache yet: Step 3's routed search will (nexus-7fku2.1.2, nexus-7fku2.1.1).
