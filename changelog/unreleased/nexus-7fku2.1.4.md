### Added

- **Engine: `GET /version` reports `vector_topic_route_supported: true` (RDR-134 Step 4).** A boolean on its own: an engine without `topic_route` ignores the key and answers an unrouted search with no status on any `per_collection` entry, so a client that wants routing reads this flag first and sends the key only when it is `true`. The name is the feature's, as the RDR spells it, not `vector_search_topic_route_supported`: that prefix belongs to `chash_allow`, which four routes share, and `topic_route` is one route's. A cloud client does not see the flag until the public edge allowlists it (paired conexus work).
- **Engine: how `topic_route` treats the request's other fields, pinned (RDR-134 Step 4).** `chash_allow` and a metadata `where` leave the whole request unrouted; `thresholds` drop routed rows as they drop unscoped ones; `lexical` is not routed (the lexical arm and its rows merge as before); `rerank`, `rerank_top_k`, `rerank_max_candidates`, `limit`, `include_embeddings`, `embeddings_limit`, `content_chars` and `include_source_uri` act on the merged rows, routed, fallback and unscoped alike; `sensitivity: "exclude"` holds inside the routed search and inside its unscoped fallback. No behaviour changes; a test now holds each of these.
