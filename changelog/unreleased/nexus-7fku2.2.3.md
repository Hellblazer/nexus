### Fixed

- **An `nx_answer` run on the single-query fast path now reports a known cost, not an unknown one.** The fast path (a matched plan that is one search) bypasses the plan runner and recorded no step, so when no request extraction ran its `cost_usd` was `None` in the structured envelope and in `nx_answer_runs`, and a spend guard that refuses unknown costs stopped on it. It now records its one `query` as a `sql` step at the known zero cost the runner records for any step that runs no prompt, so the run cost is 0.0 plus any extraction cost. A fast-path query that raises records the same step with `ok: false`, in the envelope and in telemetry. A scope conflict runs nothing and still records no step.
- Effect on reports: new fast-path rows count at 0.0 in `nx answer-runs`' average cost and in its `--steps` tables (as operator `query`, source `sql`), where they used to be left out as NULL; rows written before this release stay NULL, so an average over a window that spans it drops. `nx answer-runs --derive-budget` keeps them out of its cost population, as before, and names them as "single-query fast path" among the exclusions.
