### Changed

- **A `--lexical` search on the per-collection route (`NX_SEARCH_PER_COLLECTION=1`) now asks the engine for each collection's lexical arm by default.** `NX_SEARCH_ROUTE_LEXICAL` is the off control now: `0`, `false`, `off` or `no` (any case) sends no `lexical` field, and the leg runs on `/hybrid-search` as it did before. With the engine's shared text-gate probe (v0.1.192) and its two probe workers, the route beat `/hybrid-search` on two of three measured queries (6.08 s against 7.36 s, 4.37 s against 5.41 s) and tied on the third (engine v0.1.194, 120 collections, nine runs). Not measured: concurrent searches sharing the cluster's parallel-worker pool, and a cloud engine. The route itself is still opt-in.
- **The engine's `event=search_per_collection_lexical` log line carries `probe_workers`**, the `NX_SEARCH_ROUTE_PROBE_WORKERS` value the request's shared probe ran with, or `none` when no shared probe ran, so a slow run can be told apart from a probe that ran serially. A log field only: no HTTP body or `/version` change.
