### Changed

- **A `--lexical` search on the per-collection route (`NX_SEARCH_PER_COLLECTION=1`) now asks the engine for each collection's lexical arm by default.** `NX_SEARCH_ROUTE_LEXICAL` is the off control now: `0`, `false`, `off` or `no` (any case) sends no `lexical` field, and the leg runs on `/hybrid-search` as it did before. With the engine's shared text-gate probe (v0.1.192) and its two probe workers, the route beat `/hybrid-search` on two of three measured queries (`advisory lock` 6.08 s against 7.36 s, `resolve_active_session_id` 4.37 s against 5.41 s) and tied on `serialization` (engine v0.1.194, nine runs). The data was a synthetic 414,000-row leaf of 120 collections, three short queries, one request at a time; small collection counts, long queries, concurrent searches sharing the cluster's parallel-worker pool, and a cloud engine were not measured. One boot at the default ran the `advisory lock` probe serially and lost (9.25 s against 5.99 s), cause undetermined. The default's speed rests on the engine's shared probe: with `NX_SEARCH_ROUTE_SHARED_PROBE=0` on the engine, or a probe that falls back, a `--lexical` search returns to the per-arm cost. The route itself is still opt-in.
- **The engine's `event=search_per_collection_lexical` log line carries `probe_workers`**: the `NX_SEARCH_ROUTE_PROBE_WORKERS` setting the request's shared probe ran with (0 is serial), or `none` when no shared probe ran. It does not show whether the workers launched. Engine side: it ships with the first engine tag after engine-service-v0.1.194, and an engine at the pin does not print it. A log field only: no HTTP body or `/version` change.
