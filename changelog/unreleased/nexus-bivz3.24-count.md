### Added

- **`nx catalog repair-note-uris` counts, and repairs, note URIs that name a collection the note no longer lives in** (nexus-bivz3.24, the repair half of RDR-231 critique Issue 7; engine and client, additive). A note moved by a re-home, a collection update or a rename before RDR-231 step 4 keeps `nx-note://X/t` on a row living in `Y`; the backfill changed only the scheme, so the row stayed stale and a re-put under `Y` minted a duplicate. The new route `POST /v1/catalog/note_uri_repair` (body `{apply, limit, sample}`, all optional) returns, for the caller's tenant, the live stale rows split into rewrite candidates (no live twin and no sibling rewriting onto the same URI), merge candidates (a live twin, or two stale rows with one target), and refused rows (a file-routed physical collection, or no title part), plus the tombstoned stale rows and the `nxexp://` export copies whose target collection is not the one they live in (census only). With `apply` it rewrites up to `limit` rewrite candidates in ONE transaction: the document rows are locked, the target URIs are checked again against the live holders, and the catalog row, its aspect row and its highlight row are rewritten together, each by its own value. A holder found at the check, or one that commits before the UPDATE and trips `ux_catalog_documents_live_source_uri`, is the existing `SourceUriCollision` (409) naming the holder, with nothing written. Merge candidates are listed and never merged; which row wins is a design decision, and each sampled entry carries the row's and its twin's `chunk_count`, `indexed_at`, `has_aspects` and `has_highlights` for the rule that will choose. A `chroma://`-spelled live holder counts as a twin. `--apply` writes a ledger (`--ledger PATH`, default a timestamped `.jsonl` in the working directory) of every row changed in all three tables with its old and new URI, flushed after each batch; it is the only undo record. A chain of stale rows takes more than one pass and `--apply` repeats (capped). The `nxexp://` copies are census only: their target segment goes stale with every rename or re-home, and a repair is a follow-on. The verb is a dry run by default, always prints the census, takes `--json`, `--sample`, `--batch-size` and `--ledger`, and names an engine without the route instead of tracing. See `docs/cli-reference.md`.
