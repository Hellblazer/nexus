### Fixed

- **The aspect worker keeps a claim renewed from the moment it takes it until its last verdict** (RDR-235, nexus-bpikw). The renewer used to start after the per-claim catalog checks and stop before the loop that writes results and acks or nacks, so a claim was unrenewed from its claim to the first renewal and again from the last renewal to its write. The engine refuses renew, ack, nack and the write fence once a lease has passed, with no resurrection, so a stall in either window lapsed a claim a live worker still held: the write failed with "a write met a claim that was not live", and the task was retried. With the production 600 s lease and 120 s renewal it took a stall of about 480 s; the MVV journey's 2 s lease and 0.5 s renewal failed on a stall of 1.5 s. The renewer now starts when the batch is claimed and stops after the last write, ack or nack, and renews only claims still held, so a finished claim is not renewed into a false claim-lost warning. The renewal deadline counts from the claim instead of from the extraction; the 120 s margin on it already covered the work outside the extractor.
