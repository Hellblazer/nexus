### Fixed

- **An idle session's compaction checkpoint is no longer deleted by another session's PreCompact hook** (nexus-ct9sv). The hook pruned every checkpoint file older than 48 hours by mtime, so a session idle past that lost its unused checkpoint before its deferred compaction was used, and the restore at its next message found nothing. A current checkpoint that no compaction has used yet (no summary appended, not yet injected) is now pruned only past 7 days, and is restored up to that age; used checkpoints, archives and sidecars keep the 48-hour prune. Seven days is a chosen bound, not a measured one. The related case of a discarded idle checkpoint reused by a later compaction whose own pre failed is unchanged in code, but its window is now 7 days instead of 48 hours; the restore still states the checkpoint's real age and that it may predate the compaction.
