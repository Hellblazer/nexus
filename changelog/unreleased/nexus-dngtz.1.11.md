### Fixed

- **The session and tenant values in the engine log prefix are escaped, so a client-chosen session id cannot forge a line or a field.** `/v1/sessions/start` takes any non-blank `session_id`, and the engine printed it raw in `[cid=... tenant=... session=...]` and in the `session_token_minted` line: a newline wrote a second log line, and a space, `]` or `=` added fields a supervisor reader could match by substring. The tenant and session values in the engine log line prefix, and in the `session_token_minted`, `session_token_closed` and `t1_session_closed` lines, are now printed escaped: control characters print as `\xNN` (or `\uNNNN` above 0xFF), as do space, `[`, `]` and `=`, and a backslash is doubled. The stored id, routing and comparisons keep the raw value. RDR-167, Phase 1.
