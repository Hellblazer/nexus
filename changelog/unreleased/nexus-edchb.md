### Security

- **The `nexus_diag` role can no longer read chunk text** (nexus-edchb; engine and client; rides the next engine tag). `nexus_diag` is the read-only diagnostic role and bypasses row-level security, so it sees every tenant. To make the security-invoker counts view `nexus.diag_chash_conformance` work, the runAlways changeset `grants-nexus-diag-3` granted it SELECT on whole tables: `nexus.chunks`, `nexus.catalog_document_chunks`, `nexus.topic_assignments` and `nexus.relevance_log`. With that grant the role could read every tenant's `chunk_text` and the other content columns. It now holds SELECT on only the one column of each table that the view reads: `chunks.chash`, `catalog_document_chunks.chash`, `topic_assignments.doc_id` and `relevance_log.chunk_id`. The narrowing takes effect at the engine's next boot, which revokes the table grant (`grants-nexus-diag-2`) and grants the columns back (`-3`). Local provisioning (`nx init --service` and its re-run) also stops granting `nexus_diag` SELECT on every table of `nexus` and `t1` once the counts view exists. Before, that grant held until the engine's next boot. `nx doctor`'s chash check and the forensics topic read through the view and are unchanged.
