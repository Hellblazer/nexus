### Added

- **The chash VALIDATE job and the doctor say what a non-32-byte row is, and whose it is** (nexus-f3yqd; engine change, ships in engine-service-v0.1.190). Such a row is not expected on a migrated store: the boot migrations delete every non-32-byte chunk row (hygiene-001-5, first shipped in engine 0.1.91) and every orphan manifest row (hygiene-001-8), and the NOT VALID width CHECK refuses a new one, so a refusal means the CHECK was bypassed, or dropped and re-added after rows were written. The engine has no repair for such rows, so the reports are descriptive, change no row, and name no route. A refusal of `nexus.catalog_document_chunks` or a chunks table in the VALIDATE job's status carries `reasons` (counts per reason among the first rows in key order, at most the pre-check cap; `reasons_capped` is true only when more rows exist or a tenant was not counted), or `reasons_unavailable: true` when the session could not read every row to say. The reasons are `legacy_digest_key` (16 bytes that are the first 16 of `sha256(chunk_text)`, the pre-RDR-180 key), `text_mismatch` (16 bytes that are not), `no_text` (a reference-only chunk), `unexpected_width` (not 16 bytes) and, for the manifest, `manifest_without_chunk` (only where the manifest-to-chunk foreign key was dropped too). A chunks table's refusal also lists up to 20 bad chunks in key order (`chunks`, with `chunks_capped` exact: the list is read with one row more than it shows), each with tenant, collection, embedding model, chash, reason and `owners`: up to 5 documents (`owners_capped` says more), the documents whose manifest names the chunk (`via: manifest`) or the note stamped with its hex in `metadata.doc_id` (`via: note`); an empty list is no owner, `null` is not looked up (no scoped session, or the lookups ran out of their 10 s). The manifest refusal carries `documents`: per reason, up to 20 of the documents that hold the bad rows (tenant, doc_id, title) with an exact `capped`. `GET /v1/schema/chash-conformance?detail=legacy_chunks` adds the same for the caller's own tenant when a width CHECK is not validated (`chunk_reasons`, `manifest_reasons` with their `_capped` flags, `manifest_documents`, `chunks`, `chunks_capped`), only when asked: it scans the tenant's chunks (no index serves a width test), so each statement is bounded to 5 s and the whole detail to 8 s (`deadline_exceeded` says stages were skipped or cut, and owners not looked up are `null`), one detail at a time per tenant (a concurrent call answers `available: false, busy: true`), and the default answer still scans nothing. The doctor detail has no client caller yet. Nothing in either report changes a row, and the VALIDATE of the chunks table stays refused while any such row remains.
