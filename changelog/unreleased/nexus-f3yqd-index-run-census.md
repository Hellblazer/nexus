### Added

- **Two read-only catalog routes so `nx doctor` stops walking every document for its fence and allocator rows** (nexus-f3yqd; engine change, ships in engine-service-v0.1.190). `GET /v1/catalog/index-run/census?limit=N` answers, in one bounded read, what the stale index-run fences row and the failed-runs-with-hidden-chunks row read from a full walk (22 s on a laptop tenant): `stale` (documents stranded in `indexing` for 6 hours or more, read from the partial index on documents that are not complete), `fence` (the counts of documents with and without a fence state, the earliest completed run and the earliest batched run that fix the baseline, and the unstamped documents indexed after it) and `failed_hidden` (failed documents that have chunks stored and no manifest row, the number `nx catalog reconcile` would rebuild). Each bucket carries its own count, `capped` flag and examples, and a bucket that cannot be read inside the 10 second statement bound answers `{"timed_out": true}` while the others still answer. `GET /v1/catalog/owners/next_seq_drift?limit=N` lists the owners whose `next_seq` trails their highest child (tombstoned children included) without flooring them; it shares the high-water mark and the drift test with `POST /v1/catalog/owners/sweep_next_seq_drift`, which still floors. `/version` gains `catalog_index_run_census_supported` and `catalog_next_seq_drift_read_supported`. The client half is a separate change; until it lands the routes have no caller, and the public edge's `/version` allowlist must add the two flags before a cloud client can read them.
