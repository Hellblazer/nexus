### Fixed

- **An engine restart during `nx upgrade` no longer fails the upgrade** (nexus-f3yqd.5). When the engine stopped answering during a data step, for example a restart from another shell, the step failed and `nx upgrade` exited 1, though the engine was back seconds later. Now the step prints "the engine stopped answering (a restart?); waiting up to 120 s for it, then this step resumes", waits until the engine answers its health check again, and runs the step again. The step reads the store from the first document and skips what is already done. It waits up to three times per call, and under `--auto` never past the run's deadline. If the engine does not come back, the step is deferred and the next `nx upgrade` runs it again; later steps in the same run do not wait again. An engine that was never running is not waited for, and a slow answer, an HTTP refusal or a malformed answer still fails the step. On a managed service the wait is a 15 s pause before the retry. The RDR-192 backfill step defers on an outage as before, without the wait.
