### Fixed

- **An engine restart during `nx upgrade` no longer fails the upgrade** (nexus-f3yqd.5). When the engine stopped answering during a data step (a restart from another shell, a supervisor respawn), the step failed and `nx upgrade` exited 1, though the engine was back seconds later. Now the step prints "the engine stopped answering (a restart?); waiting up to 120 s for it, then this step resumes", waits for the engine to publish its lease again, and runs the step again. Every data step reads the store's current state, so the repeat resumes where it stopped. It waits up to three times per step. If the engine does not come back, the step is deferred: nothing is lost, and the next `nx upgrade` continues it. An engine that was never running is not waited for. Any other error still fails the step.
