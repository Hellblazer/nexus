### Added

- **The engine gains `nexus.data_moves`, the registry of background data moves (RDR-246).** One row per registered move: its DML template, driving table, chunk size, throttle, verification query, optional finishing SQL, supersession link and state. It is a global table with no row-level security, read and written only by the engine's migrating role (`nexus_admin`); `nexus_svc` holds no privilege on it where `nexus_admin` is the migrating role (in the single-role posture `nexus_svc` migrates and owns the table, and the revoke is skipped by design; the runAlways service-grant loop now skips the table, and the changeset removes the default-privilege grant an upgraded database would give it). The diagnostic role `nexus_diag` is not changed: in the legacy diagnostic-grant era it can read every `nexus` table, this one included. Schema only, no data effect (nexus-f3yqd.2.12).
