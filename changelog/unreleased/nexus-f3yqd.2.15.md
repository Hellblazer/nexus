### Added

- **A data move's failure is transient or permanent, and the registry records which (RDR-246).** The worker now classifies each failed pass. A transient failure (no free background worker slot, a missing install or role, a lost connection, a recorded chunk error with SQLSTATE 40001, 40P01, 55P03, 57014, class 53 or 57P01 to 57P03, a raised 42501, 42883 or class 08 error that dml_utils did not record, a CALL that returned without finishing its run) leaves the move `blocked:` with "retry N, last <time>: <error>", adds one to `transient_retries` and waits min(30 s * 2^(min(N, 6) - 1), 15 min) from `last_transient_at` before the next pass, read from the row so a restart changes nothing; from the 20th consecutive transient the text adds "needs an operator", and the worker never stops retrying. A pass that ends without a transient error clears the count. A permanent failure (any other recorded chunk error, a run dml_utils recorded as cancelled, a driving schema or table that does not exist, an error it cannot place) sets `failed_at` and `failure`; a failed move is never retried by this process or a later boot and does not hold up the moves behind it. A move whose failure the worker could not write to the registry stays `blocked:` rather than being reported failed (nexus-f3yqd.2.15).
- **A move's verification and completion step classify their own errors (RDR-246).** A verification is transient when it times out, loses its connection, meets a shutdown, runs out of resources (class 53) or fails with no SQLSTATE at all; a verification connection that cannot be opened is transient whatever its SQLSTATE, a login failure included. Any other error it raises, a missing function or an ungranted table included, makes the move `failed:`, since the verification text is at fault. An `on_complete` is transient when it times out, waits out its `lock_timeout`, deadlocks or fails to serialize against live writers (40P01, 40001), loses its connection, meets a shutdown or runs out of resources (class 53), because its transaction rolls back whole; any other error, one with no SQLSTATE included, makes the move `failed:` (nexus-f3yqd.2.15).
