### Added

- **The engine refuses to boot when a role that serves traffic can read every tenant's rows of a data move's driving table (RDR-246).** A data move over a tenant-scoped table other than `nexus.chunks` reads every tenant's rows as the migrating role, through an owner-read SELECT policy a changeset of its own creates `TO CURRENT_USER` with vectors-029's guard. The guard sees the roles that exist when it runs, not a membership granted later. After the schema walk, a new boot check reads the driving tables of `nexus.data_moves` on the admin connection and asks, on the serving pool, whether a permissive policy other than `tenant_isolation` applies to the serving role on any of them; if one does, the engine logs `event=driving_table_isolation_check_failed` naming the table, the policy and the role, and exits 1. A table that no longer exists passes, a completed move's table stays in scope until a changeset drops its policy, and `nexus.chunks` stays with the existing check. No move is registered yet, so nothing changes for an existing install (nexus-f3yqd.2.16).
