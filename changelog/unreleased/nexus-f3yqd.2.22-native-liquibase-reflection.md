### Fixed

- **The native engine applies the vendored `ddl_utils` and `dml_utils` libraries.** On a Postgres bundle with `pg_background` it logged `data_move_libraries_failed ... MissingReflectionRegistrationError: Cannot reflectively invoke method ... SQLFileChange.setPath` and applied neither library; the JVM engine was unaffected. The native-image metadata held the Liquibase change classes the libraries use (`SQLFileChange`, `CreateProcedureChange`, `RawSQLChange`, `AbstractSQLChange`) and the `<dbms>` precondition with `<init>` only. They now register all their declared and public methods in `reflect-config.json`, which covers the setters Liquibase calls while parsing and the getters it calls while recording and checksumming a changeset. A new JVM test loads every changelog the engine applies and fails on any read or write method Liquibase's own metadata reaches that the native metadata lacks.
