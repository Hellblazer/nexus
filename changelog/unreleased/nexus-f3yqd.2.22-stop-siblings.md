### Fixed

- **`nx daemon service stop` no longer signals wrappers that only carry the supervisor's command, or another install's engine on the same jar.** Two siblings of the jar-match fix. The supervisor match now needs an nx entry point at the head of the command, so `sudo -n lab-hand-exec ... nx daemon service start --config-dir X`, `lab-run -- ...`, `ssh h ...` and `bash -c "..."` are left alone (a root-owned one met EPERM on both signals and the stop exited 1 before its PostgreSQL step); an nx path with a space, on POSIX or Windows, still matches. On POSIX a `NEXUS_SERVICE_JAR` engine is now placed by its own `NEXUS_CONFIG_DIR`, as a bare `nx daemon service start` already was, so two installs sharing one jar path no longer sweep each other; one whose environment cannot be read is left alone, reported, and refused by the `nx daemon service pg-upgrade` idle check. Windows has no environment reader, so a jar engine there is still taken as this directory's.
- **`nx daemon service stop` says plainly when it identified a service process it could not signal.** A process of this directory's stack that another user owns (both signals fail with EPERM) is reported as identified but not signalled, apart from "survived SIGKILL" and from processes the stop could not identify at all. The stop still exits 1 before `--with-pg` reaches PostgreSQL for it; only a process it could not identify warns and lets the stop go on.
