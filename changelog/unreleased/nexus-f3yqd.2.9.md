### Added

- **Provisioning now prepares Postgres for background data moves (RDR-246).** `nx init --service` and every service start create the `pg_background` extension, the NOLOGIN NOBYPASSRLS roles `dml_utils_owner`, `dml_utils_caller` and `ddl_utils_caller`, their grants (so the engine's `nexus_admin` can `SET ROLE dml_utils_owner`) and a `liquibase` schema owned by `dml_utils_owner`. An install provisioned before this change gets them on its next start. A bundle without the pg_background library, or a `liquibase` schema that already holds someone else's tables, is logged with its reason and never fails the start. `docs/configuration.md` gives a bring-your-own-Postgres DBA the SQL for the same step.
