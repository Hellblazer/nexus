### Changed

- **`nx doctor` reads its index-run and allocator rows from the engine's census routes instead of walking every document** (nexus-f3yqd.6, F30). Against an engine that advertises `catalog_index_run_census_supported` and `catalog_next_seq_drift_read_supported` (0.1.190), the stale index-run fences, failed-runs-with-hidden-chunks and tumbler allocator drift rows run in the default sweep from two bounded requests (`GET /v1/catalog/index-run/census`, `GET /v1/catalog/owners/next_seq_drift`) and no catalog walk, and `nx doctor --check-index-runs` reads the same routes. The rows say what the walk's rows said, through the same rendering. A count the engine capped reads "at least", and a capped failed-runs census that found nothing reads "not fully checked", never "none". Whatever the engine cannot answer (a bucket that timed out, a 404 or 503, a malformed body) is a not-checked row with no warning. The walk is gone: against an older engine, or a public edge whose `/version` allowlist does not list the two flags, the three rows report not checked and say they need engine 0.1.190 or later, and `nx doctor --check-index-runs`, which now runs only those three rows, needs engine 0.1.190 too.
