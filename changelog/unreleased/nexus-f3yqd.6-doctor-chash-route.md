### Changed

- **`nx doctor`'s chash conformance checks read the engine's constraint state before scanning** (nexus-f3yqd.6, F30). Against an engine that advertises `chash_conformance_constraints_supported` (0.1.189), doctor asks `GET /v1/schema/chash-conformance` whether the chash width CHECKs on `nexus.chunks` and `nexus.catalog_document_chunks` are present and validated. When they are, no row of either table can be non-conformant, so the local poison check and the managed per-dimension report skip their scans and report clean. The legacy-debt probe over topic_assignments, frecency and relevance_log has no CHECK to rely on and still runs. Any other answer, or an older engine, scans as before with no warning.
