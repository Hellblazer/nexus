### Added

- **A seat's own T2 notes (`identity/<id>`) show up at SessionStart in one line.** `Your notes (identity/i5): a, b, c (+2 more)`: up to five titles of the bound identity's project, newest first, at most 300 bytes; nothing for an empty project, a session with no identity, an error, or a read that runs past 2 s. It is a title list, never a get or search. The identity comes from the token file that lists the session, or from the hook's own Claude ancestor when the bind has not listed a new session id yet. The conexus `nexus` skill gains a where-it-goes table. `nx memory curate` (`--t2`, `--cold`) now covers the `identity/*` projects, and a transcript read of `identity/<id>/<title>` counts for the project `identity/<id>`. Unused by the 8.5.3 release date plus 30 days, by transcript `memory_get` reads, the guidance is retired (RDR-253 Step 6 part 3, nexus-hczdo).
- **The client refuses a write to `identity/<id>` unless the caller is that seat and the project is its own.** An ephemeral or delegated identity (known from its token file even when its token has lapsed), a seat writing another seat's project, and a caller whose kind the engine did not answer are refused in one line before any request, by `put`, `put_or_merge`, `put_replacing`, `import_entry`, `import_entries_batch`, `delete` by project and title, and `insert_summary`. The refusal is the client's only: the engine fences just `threads`, so a raw HTTP caller is not stopped, and the id-keyed paths (`delete` by id, `merge_memories`, `restore`) are not covered (nexus-hczdo).
