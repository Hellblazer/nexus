### Added

- **A seat's own T2 notes (`identity/<id>`) show up at SessionStart, and only a seat may write them.** SessionStart prints one line, `Your notes (identity/i5): a, b, c (+2 more)`: up to five titles of the bound identity's project, newest first, nothing for an empty project or a session with no identity, and nothing on an error or after a 2 s budget (a title list, so no entry's `access_count` moves). A write to `identity/<id>` from an ephemeral or delegated identity is refused by the client in one line before any request (`HttpMemoryStore.put`, `put_or_merge`, `put_replacing`; the engine fences only `threads`); `nx memory put --mine` is refused for a delegated child, which can still read its parent's notes with `--mine`. The conexus `nexus` skill gains a where-it-goes table. Unused after 30 days, the guidance is retired (RDR-253 Step 6 part 3, nexus-hczdo).
