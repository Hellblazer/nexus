### Fixed

- **Registered work rows now end with their work, so a restart never needs anyone to mark them by hand.** The SubagentStop hook and the `nx ci wait`, `nx tuple wait` and `nx identity work run` labels retry a failed end once on a longer bound, and log one that still fails. A new reap closes a running row whose work has ended (its process is gone, it predates the session, or its worktree is gone), the way its registrar would have, and logs a count. Every SessionStart reaps the session's own rows in a detached child, `nx identity work list --reap` reaps before it lists, and `claude-restart-sessions` reads with `--reap`. The restart script retries a work read that times out (5 s, then 30 s) and names why a list could not be read. It holds that one session back instead of refusing the whole batch. The readiness ask no longer tells sessions to mark stale rows done (nexus-hczdo).
