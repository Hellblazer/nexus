### Added

- **Engine records and routes for session identity (RDR-253 Step 1).** `/v1/identity` creates seats (named, long-lived identities), ephemeral and delegated identities, and binds a process to one under a compare-and-set on its generation, so two simultaneous binds cannot both win. It records each take, keep and loss in an append-only binding log, indexes sessions, renews a binding and lapses it after 300 seconds without renewal, records a holder reported gone, renames a seat (retiring the old alias for 7 days of notice and then for good), and removes an identity's state under an operator purge that only the tenant credential reaches. Ephemeral and delegated identities are tombstoned 30 days after they end or lapse with their holder gone, by the scheduled tuple sweep. New tables `identity_counters`, `identity_records`, `identity_aliases`, `identity_bindings`, `identity_sessions`, `identity_memberships` and `identity_work` (schema only, nothing migrated at boot); memberships keep a group's roster in join order until the identity leaves or is tombstoned, and have routes to join, leave and list; work an identity owns is registered and changed in place on `identity_work`; the `mailbox/<address>` template accepts `address_kind: identity`. A tenant is registered for the scheduled sweep when its first identity is created. No client uses any of it yet.
