### Fixed

- **`claude-restart-sessions` mails its asks to the target's identity mailbox, since an 8.6 session drains only that one.** The handoff ask, the ready ask and the wake went to `mailbox/<session id>` with `address_kind=session`, which no 8.6 session reads. The script now finds the target's identity (`nx identity show --session`, once per session id) and sends `address_kind=identity`. A session that holds no identity is not mailed, and the existing typed fallback of each ask runs. A withdrawal drops the ask's marker and claims nothing: the engine lets only an identity's own token claim from its mailbox, so a surviving copy of the row reads as forged and expires with its TTL. That copy can still reach the session as a turn until it expires (a handoff ask lasts its timeout plus 5 minutes, a ready ask 20, a wake 30): it is inert because the session checks the ask's marker before acting on it, not because anything removes the row. Until a session claims it, the row is re-announced every 150 seconds, up to five times, so an idle session can spend a turn declining it. A mailed wake now lives three minutes (its 60-second busy wait plus two) instead of 30, so a wake that fell back to typing leaves no copy for long. Taking a row back out needs an engine route that lets a sender withdraw its own mail (nexus-hczdo.2.1).
