### Added

- **`nx ci ack --not-mine <sha> [--note TEXT]` lets the owner of a red develop say it is not theirs.** It posts a `kind=disclaim` row on the same `--owner` subspace an ack goes to, with the same sha handling, owner check and identity stamping (`--base` names the board of any repo; without it the repo comes from the `origin` remote and the command uses that repo's one board with an open red, or refuses and names `--base`; `nx ci ack` takes the same options and no longer assumes nexus's board), and the optional note as its body. The engine does not act on it yet, so it is recorded only; from the engine cut that reads it, a disclaim from the current owner hands the red on at once. `nx group ack-times` lists a red's disclaims and never counts one as an ack (RDR-252 post-close audit S1, nexus-igea9).
