### Fixed

- **Engine: an acknowledged red no longer escalates when the fail-safe's identity read fails (RDR-252).** When a deadline's evaluation and then the fail-safe's liveness read both failed, the fail-safe read nobody as live, could not match the owner's ack to the row, and posted an `escalated` row with `owner` null that woke every member, although the owner had acknowledged. Now, if the read fails and the owner's stamped ack sits on the row's own tip (posted at or after the row, with a sender session, its `from` the row's owner; a non-owner's ack and an ack with no sender session do not count), the fail-safe throws and the deadline timer retries after 30 seconds, without end; the twelfth failure of a topic's hand-off since its last success, cancel or eviction, and every twelfth after it, raises the error-level log event `board_ack_deadline_stuck` (it posts no row). Known transition: an ack row written before engine 0.1.200 has a typed `from` and does not match, so under this compound fault an open red acknowledged before the deploy escalates with `owner` null, a bounded false wake. If the read fails and no such ack exists, nobody acknowledged, and the `escalated` row is posted at once with `owner` null, as for nobody live. The fail-safe makes the read only when an escalation is owed, and the timer logs a failing hand-off's stack on its first attempt only.
