### Added

- **`claude-restart-sessions --ask-ready --group <name>` and `--status --group <name>`.** The ready check becomes a group operation (RDR-252): it asks, and reports, only the live sessions whose names are the aliases of the group's live members, read from the engine roster (`nx group list <name> --json`). It prints how many members it covers and each one it does not, with its state and why (no live session here, or no alias), so a ready exit cannot hide who was never asked. A blank name, and a group that is unknown, disbanded, or without a live member here, refuse in one line. The restart itself never takes a group, and a group round replaces the round file, so a restart after it still names the sessions it never asked (nexus-igea9).
