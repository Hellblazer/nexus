### Fixed

- **A skipped or failed develop push record is now in the push summary, a failed stamped write is retried as a minimal unstamped record, and `from` names the pusher.** The record is written by the new `nx ci record-push` (any repo's push script can call it; the board base is `--base` or derived from the remote URL and branch, and refused when it cannot be, never defaulted). `scripts/git-push-develop.sh` prints `PUSH_RECORD_SKIPPED`, `PUSH_RECORD_FALLBACK` or `PUSH_RECORD_FAILED` on stdout after `PUSH_OK` (the stderr line stays), `from` is the pusher's identity id (or `unknown`), and the retry keeps the base so the chain keeps its link (nexus-igea9, RDR-252 post-close audit S3).
- **Pushes to develop are held while a red is open.** The push script asks the new `nx ci check-hold` first and refuses with `PUSH_REFUSED_DEVELOP_RED` (exit 13) unless the pusher owns the red or pushed one of its suspects; `--red-fix '<reason>'` pushes past it and the reason is written into the push record body (`red_fix`). A failed read of the red warns and does not refuse (nexus-igea9, RDR-252 post-close audit, hold-your-pushes).
