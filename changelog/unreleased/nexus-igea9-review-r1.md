- **The develop-red hold and the push record are sturdier (RDR-252 review round 1).** `nx ci check-hold` marks an override with one exact `PUSH_RED_FIX_APPLIED` line (and a flag nothing needed as `PUSH_RED_FIX_UNNEEDED`), so the push script no longer matches free text; an identity that cannot be read warns and never refuses, a genuine no identity is refused with a pointer to `nx identity show`, and a suspect with no attributable push record is named with the way out. `nx ci record-push` cuts the `red_fix` reason by UTF-8 bytes to fit the board's 1024-byte body cap (control characters become spaces, the fallback record keeps it), says whether the record is stamped (`PUSH_RECORD_UNSTAMPED` when nothing stamped it), and `git-push-develop.sh` falls back to the legacy `nx tuple out` record when the installed nx has no `ci record-push` (`PUSH_RECORD_LEGACY`).
