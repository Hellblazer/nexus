### Added

- **`scripts/git-push-develop.sh` writes a push record after every push to develop (RDR-252 Step 2).** After `PUSH_OK`, on every path including `--skip-lock`, it posts one tuple to `board/ci/nexus-develop--push`: dims `from`, `kind=push` and `sha=<tip>`, and the body `{"base": <the remote develop tip the push replaced>, "n": <commits>}`. RDR-252's owner stamper reads these records to order develop's tips and to map each push to the identity that made it, through the record's stamp; `from` is a label and says `unknown` until a shell verb for the RDR-253 identity exists. With `--skip-lock` the installed `nx` is resolved for this write; if none is found, no record is written. The record never fails a push: an error is one `PUSH_RECORD_FAILED` (or `PUSH_RECORD_SKIPPED`) line on stderr, stdout keeps its single `PUSH_OK` line, and the exit code is the push's. A push to any other branch writes no record.
