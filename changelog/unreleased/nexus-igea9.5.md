### Added

- **Group records for RDR-252, in the engine (Step 1).** `/v1/group` forms, reads, updates, disbands and (with the tenant credential only) overrides a group: one permanent row per name in the new table `group_records` (schema only, nothing migrated at boot) with its leader, purpose, end time, acknowledgement deadlines (whole minutes from 5 to 240, one default and one per board topic) and the board topics it `serves`. Only the leader's identity token writes the record, at the leader's current generation; a write with no token is refused even when the leader's seat is unleased. A first or new leader is a live, leased seat, and a new leader is already a member. One live group serves a topic, held by a per-topic advisory lock that the owner stamper will share. A disbanded name is re-formed in place, and the disband, re-form and override are written to `gc_audit`. Three rules join RDR-253's membership routes for a group that has a record: only a seat joins, a disbanded group takes no joins, and the leader does not leave. A `board-ci`-scoped write to a `board/ci/<topic>` subspace whose topic ends `--owner` or `--push`, or whose `topic` key differs from the subspace, now gets 403, and the template's doc block names the new writers and the body fields of push, owner, clear and ack rows. No client uses any of it yet.
