### Added

- **The engine names who owns develop's red.** On each `board/ci/<base>--done` write whose verdict is `success` or `failure`, the engine works out the branch's state in commit order from the push records on `--push` (a cancelled run is no verdict, and a tip it leaves untested is a suspect) and posts at most one row to `board/ci/<base>--owner` as `from=engine`: an owner row naming the pusher of the earliest suspect (the group's leader with a named reason when that pusher is not live, not a member, unrecorded, or the chain has a gap), the next up, the first ten suspects and their count; a refreshed row when a newer red has the same earliest suspect; a clear row when the topic goes green. Only a topic a live group serves gets owner rows; a green still clears an open one. A per-topic advisory lock serializes the stamper with its deadline timer. If the owner has not acknowledged by the group's `ack_deadline` (or its per-topic override), the engine hands the red to next up once, then escalates with one `escalated` row naming the leader; the timer is rebuilt from the owner rows after a restart. `IdentityRepository.liveness` reads an identity's generation, state and lease with the 300-second rule applied and writes no lapse row (RDR-252 Step 3).
