### Added

- **`nx group` and `nexus.groups`: the client of the engine's group records (RDR-252 Step 4, part B1).** `nx group form NAME --serves TOPIC` makes the calling session's identity the leader of a new group (and re-forms a disbanded name); `set` changes the leader, `--ack-deadline`, `--ack-deadline-for TOPIC=MINUTES` (merged into the stored per-topic deadlines), `--ends` and `--serves`; `disband` ends it; `override` (tenant credential, no identity token) replaces the leader or ends the group while its leader cannot write. `join` and `leave` add and remove the caller's membership, refuse an ephemeral or delegated identity and a leader that has not handed the group on, and print that the MCP server must reconnect, because membership is read once when a server starts. `list NAME` shows the leader and the members in join order by alias, each with its RDR-253 state (`unheld`, `live`, `lapsed`, `ended`, or `unleased`), and `ack-times NAME` is the Day 2 calibration read: per red on the topics the group serves, the sha, the time its episode began, the time of the acknowledgement the engine would count (the owner's, never one from an identity the red was handed away from) and the minutes between. Group and membership writes carry the session's identity token, with the one-time resend on a 401 that a threads write already has. `nexus.groups` is the same client for the aura load stage: `read_group(client, name)` returns the group record or `None` when the name has no record.
