### Added

- **`nx aura check` and `nx aura list`, with the on/off resolver** (nexus-mdbds.10.2, RDR-249 Phase 1 Step 2). `nx aura check [NAME]` checks every aura file against what a file cannot check alone: its procedure and header, the argument mapping (every required argument mapped, none mapped that the header lacks), a `[clear]` table that a post could meet together with `trigger.match`, two auras writing one `display.threads` block, and the trigger and clear fields and `dims.`/`body.` sources against the engine's tuple registry. A repo file that shadows a shipped aura and a file whose name breaks the rule are reported, not dropped. Without the engine the dictionary checks are skipped by name and the rest run; a skip is never a pass. `nx aura list [--json]` shows name, summary, trigger, on or off, and the deciding level (default, global config, repo config or env). Every aura is off by default: `auras: {<name>: on}` in `config.yml` turns one on, `.nexus.yml` overrides it, and `NX_AURAS_OFF` (`all` or a list of names) can only turn auras off. `nexus.lua_runtime.library` gains `scan_files`, a structured enumeration; `find`, `procedures` and the `.lua` behavior are unchanged. Nothing runs an aura yet.
