### Added

- **Auras run** (nexus-mdbds.11.1, RDR-249 Phase 2 Step 1). The MCP server loads the auras turned on for a session when Claude Code's session registry first names the session. A host with no registry loads none, and so does a `claude -p` child that carries its parent's session id. Each aura's trigger topic is the aura's own subscription: it does not appear in the channel status record's board topics, so the Stop-hook CI wake guard does not count it, and a row on a topic only an aura holds is not announced to the agent. A matching post runs the aura's procedure in the Lua sandbox on a worker thread, never on the waiter's loop. When the aura shows, its `text` goes to the session's threads entry as one `## aura: <threads value>` block with one `state:` line, kept to printable ASCII and 200 characters. The entry is found by its `session:` line. None is ever created, and when two entries carry the line nothing is written. With `display.channel = true` the channel also gets a fixed notice naming the aura, the subspace and the tuple id, and nothing else; it is sent even when no threads entry could be written. A post that matches the aura's `[clear]` removes the block. Each aura runs one item at a time from its own queue. Past 20 waiting items the oldest fire is dropped, and a clear never is. Three failures in a row disable the aura for the session, and its block says so; a busy sandbox is retried, not counted. An aura whose `[clear]` a post could meet together with its trigger is refused at load, as is a second aura writing the same threads block and an aura past the 32-topic limit; aura topics count toward the session's 32. `display.threads` must now be printable ASCII of at most 80 characters.
