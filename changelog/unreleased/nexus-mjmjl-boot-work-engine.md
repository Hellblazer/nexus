### Added

- **`nx upgrade`, `nx doctor` and `nx daemon service status` say what heavy work the engine is doing** (nexus-f3yqd.3). Engine-service 0.1.187 reports its long-running work, such as a build of per-collection vector indexes, in `GET /v1/status` as `boot_work`. The client now reads it once the engine is serving: before a wait on the engine, `nx upgrade` and `nx doctor` print what it is doing, how far along it is and what stopping costs. `nx daemon service status` lists it after the service details, and `--json` carries it as `boot_work`. Before the engine binds, the supervisor's migration record still reports the work as before. An engine that predates the field, or a local install with no running service, shows nothing.
