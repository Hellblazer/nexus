### Fixed

- **`nx self install` no longer sits silent for up to ten minutes on uv's cache prune** (nexus-mjmjl). The prune after a finished install waited on uv's cache lock until its 600 s bound ran out whenever another uv process was reading the cache (any running `uv run` or `uvx` is enough). It now says it is cleanup before it starts, waits at most 15 s for the lock (`UV_LOCK_TIMEOUT`), and when the cache is busy moves the prune to a detached background process that runs once the other uv process ends, or gives up after 6 h; its output goes to `<config>/logs/uv-cache-prune.log`, and a second install does not start another while one waits. `nx self gc --prune-uv-cache` prunes the same way.
- **A command that waits on a migrating engine says so** (nexus-mjmjl). While the engine runs its schema migration (20 minutes or more on a large store, with nothing answering), the storage-service supervisor keeps `<config>/service/migration-status.json` up to date and removes it as soon as the migration phase or the wait ends. `nx doctor` (on stderr) and `nx upgrade` (on stdout; to the log under `--auto`) print "The engine is migrating its database schema (at <changeset>, <n> changeset(s) left), running for <time since the migration began>" before they start, and the lease waits of `nx init` and of every command that starts the service (a detached supervisor or an autostart unit) print it while they wait, every 30 s. Interrupting the command does not stop the migration, and the line says what stopping the service would do. When such a wait gives up after 60 s, its error says the migration is still running and to run the command again once it finishes, in place of "did not become ready". During the per-model copy (`vectors-030-1`, which runs before the engine answers anything) the notice also names the step it is on ("copying chunks into the per-model tables", "checking the copy row by row", ...), shows "about N% copied (estimate)" during the copy (database growth since the step began over the source table's size, read every 30 s, capped at 99%), and says "Do not stop nexus: stopping restarts this step from the beginning. Your data is safe." A record not refreshed for 90 s is ignored. The record has the shape of the engine's coming `/v1/status` `boot_work` entries (nexus-f3yqd.3).
- **Downloads show size, rate and time left** (nexus-mjmjl). An engine or PostgreSQL bundle download of a megabyte or more reports on stderr: one line redrawn in place on a terminal (no escape sequences, so a legacy Windows console shows it too), plain lines every 15 s otherwise (a hook's log), and `downloaded <name>: <size> in <time> (<rate>)` at the end. On Linux and macOS, the uv build of a new generation in an interactive `nx self install`, whose own output names each large package as it downloads, now adds a line every 15 s with the packages and megabytes done, the rate and the time left for those announced, or that the build is still running. The bge, cross-encoder and MinerU model downloads are not covered yet.
- **`nx upgrade`, `nx self install` and `nx doctor` say what they are doing, step by step** (nexus-mjmjl). `nx upgrade` prints `Step N of M: <what> (<what it may involve>)` before each step, a line with the elapsed time every 5 s while a step runs (30 s when the output is not a terminal), `done in <time>` or `failed after <time>: <first line of the error, credentials redacted>`, and a summary of every step's duration; under `--auto` stdout stays quiet and the same lines go to stderr, which the session hook writes to `<config>/logs/upgrade-auto-child.log`. `nx self install` reports its build and cache-prune steps the same way, and so does `nx init`'s local first run (provision and start the service, run the ladder, seed the plan library); a refusal before any work stays one line. `nx doctor` names each check on stderr as it runs (redrawn in place on a terminal, from the first second elsewhere), shows the elapsed time of a check past 3 s, includes its engine status read, catalog document walk and supplementary checks, and lists the checks that took a second or more at the end; `--json` stdout is unchanged. The first command after conexus changes version prints "conexus changed from X to Y: finishing the switch first ..." on stderr with a line every 4 s, before it restarts old-version processes or converges the engine, instead of working silently.
