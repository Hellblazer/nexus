### Fixed

- **`nx collection delete` says why when a re-embed job holds the collection.** Engine 0.1.202 refuses to delete a collection while a re-embed job that has not ended names it as its source or target, and removes nothing. The command now names the job and says to wait for it to finish (`nx collection re-embed --status JOB`) or cancel it (`nx collection re-embed --abort JOB`), and exits non-zero, instead of reporting an unconfirmed delete.
- **`nx collection re-embed --all` no longer fails on a re-entry the engine would refuse.** A kept-tombstoned job whose target collection was retired or removed cannot be re-entered; the run now prints one line (`A: not re-entered: its target B is retired`) and carries on, and does the same if the engine's refusal arrives after the check, instead of exiting 1.
- **`nx t3 quarantine restore` explains a restore the engine refuses for an unregistered origin, and the index walk goes on.** When quarantined chunks name an origin collection with no registry row, the engine will not re-create it from its label and restores nothing. The command says which collection and how many chunks stay quarantined (exit 5, as for other engine refusals). `nx index` logs it once for that collection, skips its prune for the pass, and continues with the next collection rather than logging a failed prune with a traceback.
