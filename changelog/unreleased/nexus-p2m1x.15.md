### Changed

- **Engine: a collection move no longer rewrites `chroma://` residue or an `nx-note://` value with a file-routed segment (RDR-229 P2.10).** A rename, a re-home, a re-embed move, a collection update and a reconcile commit that repoints a document rewrite its `source_uri` only when it is an `nx-note://` URI of a note, that is, when the collection it leaves has no `rdr__`, `docs__` or `code__` prefix (`NoteUri.moveTo`, `NoteUri.namesCollection`, and `nexus.catalog_reconcile_commit`, redefined by `catalog-049-1`). A legacy `chroma://` note is no longer moved; the stale-note repair rewrites it to `nx-note://<collection>/`. Moving residue onto a prefix-free label would have turned it into a note.
- **Engine: residue that already moved onto a prefix-free segment is repaired in the background (RDR-229 P2.10).** `catalog-049-2` registers the data move `rdr229-residue-catalog-documents`; its `on_complete` registers `rdr229-residue-document-aspects` and `rdr229-residue-document-highlights`. A `chroma://S/rest` value whose segment has no file-routed prefix, on a row filed under `S` whose collection has content type `code`, `docs` or `rdr`, becomes `chroma://<content_type>__S/rest`; a row whose new value a live document holds is skipped, and so are its satellites. Nothing is rewritten at boot, no schema changes, and `catalog-050` adds the three owner-read policies the moves need. Sized on a production PITR fork (chunk 1000, `sleep_ms` 100 and 50, statement timeout 30 s; the fork held no residue, so the timing is the walk alone).
- **`nx catalog backfill-source-uri` reads the residue's own segment.** A `chroma://` row is a candidate when its own segment has a `rdr__`, `docs__` or `code__` prefix or its collection has content type `code`, `docs` or `rdr`, and the path is the text after that segment, so residue a move left behind, and residue the repair move rewrote, is re-derived to its `file://` identity instead of refused as malformed.
