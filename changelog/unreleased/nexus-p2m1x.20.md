### Added

- **The engine builds the unique id indexes of `nexus.chunks` and `nexus.taxonomy_centroids` in the background, and a new leaf gets one at creation.** RDR-229 P3.3b. After the engine serves, and only once the boot-time completion check reports every stamp move and every P3.3a validation move complete, a pass of its own (not the PCI reconciler's, so it runs with `NX_SEARCH_PCI` on or off and outside the per-leaf cap) builds, one at a time with `CREATE UNIQUE INDEX CONCURRENTLY`, a unique index on `(tenant_id, collection_id, <key>, embedding_model)` for every leaf, drops and rebuilds an INVALID leftover, attaches the leaf, model-partition and table constraints, and last registers the table's swap data move (`rdr229-swap-chunks`, `rdr229-swap-taxonomy_centroids`), once. On an install whose data moves are blocked the pass builds nothing and registers nothing. `/v1/status` `boot_work` shows `leaf-index-pass` while it has indexes to build. `nexus.partition_ensure_leaf` (vectors-073) creates the same index on a leaf made while the table has no id constraint yet; with the constraint PostgreSQL's own index serves.
