### Changed

- **RDR-229 P3.3c: the two swap moves.** The moves `rdr229-swap-chunks` and `rdr229-swap-taxonomy_centroids` that P3.3b's pass registers now carry their work. Their verification (READ ONLY, ACCESS SHARE, under `verify_statement_timeout_ms` 60000) returns a row for each leaf without a valid unique index on the id key, and for the chunks swap reads `nexus.chunks` (every leaf heap and the id-key indexes) and `nexus.catalog_collections` once with a plain scan so the completion, which keeps `statement_timeout_ms` 1400 and a `lock_timeout` of 700 ms, finds them cached. Their `on_complete` refuses when the id unique key is missing (SQLSTATE 55000, permanent). The chunks swap then adds the three child id keys `fk_catalog_chunks_chunk_id`, `topic_assignments_chunk_id_fk` and `chunk_orphaned_at_chunk_id_fk` (`NOT VALID`, `ON UPDATE CASCADE`, each with its name key's ON DELETE action) into the chunks id key, registers the three moves `rdr229-fkvalidate-<table>-chunk-fk` that validate them, and adds and validates the RDR-225 composite `chunks_collection_id_fk` on `(tenant_id, collection_id, embedding_model)` directly (PostgreSQL takes no `NOT VALID` on a partitioned referencing table). The centroids swap adds nothing beyond the check. Nothing is dropped: the name primary keys, the three name child keys and `chunks_collection_fk` stay until P3.7 and P4.1. A lock the completion cannot get in 700 ms is a transient `blocked:` (55P03) and the move retries (nexus-p2m1x.21).
