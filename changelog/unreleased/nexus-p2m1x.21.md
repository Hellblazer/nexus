### Changed

- **RDR-229 P3.3c: the two swap moves.** The moves `rdr229-swap-chunks` and `rdr229-swap-taxonomy_centroids` that P3.3b's pass registers now carry their work. Their verification (READ ONLY, ACCESS SHARE, under `verify_statement_timeout_ms` 60000) returns a row for each leaf without a valid unique index on the id key (a catalog read; the completion keeps `statement_timeout_ms` 1400 and a `lock_timeout` of 700 ms). Their `on_complete` refuses when the id unique key is missing (SQLSTATE 55000, permanent). The chunks swap then adds the three child id keys `fk_catalog_chunks_chunk_id`, `topic_assignments_chunk_id_fk` and `chunk_orphaned_at_chunk_id_fk` (`NOT VALID`, `ON UPDATE CASCADE`, each with its name key's ON DELETE action; the manifest's is `DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED`, so `purge_trash` and the collection delete, which defer only the name key and delete chunks before their manifest rows, still commit) into the chunks id key, registers the three moves `rdr229-fkvalidate-<table>-chunk-fk` that validate them, and queues the RDR-225 composite `chunks_collection_id_fk` on `(tenant_id, collection_id, embedding_model)` (a key per leaf, then the parent key; see the next entry). The centroids swap adds nothing beyond the check. Nothing is dropped: the name primary keys, the three name child keys and `chunks_collection_fk` stay until P3.7 and P4.1. The boot isolation check also asks about `nexus.catalog_collections` while a swap move is registered. A lock the completion cannot get in 700 ms is a transient `blocked:` (55P03) and the move retries (nexus-p2m1x.21).
- **The chunks swap's verification does not look for a null id.** RDR-229 item 6 says the verification also returns any row with a null id; it deliberately does not, because gate 1 requires P3.1's NOT NULL CHECK to be validated before the pass registers the swap, and the arm would cost another scan inside the re-check (nexus-p2m1x.21).
- **The chunks swap no longer adds the composite foreign key in one validating statement; it reaches it leaf by leaf.** `chunks_collection_id_fk` from `nexus.chunks` to the collection registry used to be added by the swap's `on_complete` on the partitioned parent, where PostgreSQL 17 validates it in the statement (no `NOT VALID` on a partitioned table): SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE on chunks, every leaf and the registry for a scan that grows with the rows, inside the swap's 1400 ms bound. Now the swap's `on_complete` stays catalog-only under that bound: each leaf of chunks gets its own key (`<leaf>_cid_fk`, `NOT VALID`, the same definition as the parent's), and the swap registers one validation move per leaf (`rdr229-fkvalidate-chunks-leaf-<leaf>`) and one adoption move (`rdr229-fkadopt-chunks`). Each leaf validates in a transaction of its own, before its move's claim, holding no move row (SHARE UPDATE EXCLUSIVE on the leaf, ROW SHARE on the registry: no writer waits; the leaf's SHARE UPDATE EXCLUSIVE excludes a `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY` on that leaf and autovacuum of it for its duration, and the transaction's snapshot holds back dead-tuple cleanup), so a restart loses at most the leaf in flight and redoes it. The adoption move looks before it locks: from the catalog it checks that every leaf has a validated key matching the composite exactly (columns and order, referenced key, delete and update actions, match type, deferrability, no column list or PERIOD; a key already attached to the parent counts). While a leaf is not ready it takes no lock on chunks and ends no index builder: it queues the leaf (adds its key `NOT VALID`, registers or re-opens its validation move) and re-opens itself once, or, with the work already queued, raises 55P03 and backs off like any move (30 s doubling to 15 min); a leaf validation move that is failed or superseded fails the adoption loud naming it (an operator re-arms the leaf move first, then the adoption move, by clearing their `failed_at` and `failure`), and a paused one blocks it with 55P03 saying so. Only when every leaf looks ready does it take SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE on chunks and every leaf, under its own 200 ms lock wait (a timeout backs off and retries), check again under the lock (a leaf is created with ACCESS EXCLUSIVE on its model partition, which that lock conflicts with, so none appears after the check), and add the parent key under the tight bound. A running index build holds SHARE UPDATE EXCLUSIVE on a leaf, which that lock cannot get past, so when the lock times out the finisher looks at who held it: if every blocker is an index builder of this engine (the builder's name, this database, this role) it ends exactly those, logs which (`event=rdr229_adoption_ended_builders`: the pids, the leaves, the indexes), and leaves the failure to be the 55P03 whose backoff leads to the next attempt, which takes the lock; a blocker that is not a builder (a writer, autovacuum, a person's session) is never ended, and a pass that deferred for a leaf never ends anything. A leaf key that cannot get its own lock waits 200 ms too. PostgreSQL 17 adopts the leaf keys with no scan (measured: about 2 ms and no tuple read on a table with a 2M-row leaf; 48 ms at 303 leaves and 307 ms at 1003 leaves on a local PG 17.11, warm), but the ADD takes SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE on chunks and every leaf and ACCESS EXCLUSIVE on `nexus.catalog_collections` until commit, so registry reads and the foreign-key check of every chunk insert queue behind it for those milliseconds (the lock wait is 200 ms; a longer one fails 55P03 and the move retries). The adoption's cost grows faster than the number of leaves: 52 ms at 303 leaves and 307 ms at 1003 leaves on a local warm PG 17.11 (its two checks of the leaves included). Above about 2000 leaves it times out within its tight bound (1400 ms): the move stays open and blocks nothing (writes proceed after each timeout), each timeout is a counted retry, and after twenty of them the needs-operator flag fires with a reason that names the bound and the leaf count ("adoption timed out at 1400 ms over N leaves; raise statement_timeout_ms ... for one attempt in a quiet window"). The remedy is to raise `statement_timeout_ms` on `rdr229-fkadopt-chunks` in `nexus.data_moves` for ONE attempt in a quiet window, knowing it lengthens the write block on chunks and the registry by about that much, and then to set it back. The swap's own `on_complete` costs about 0.3 to 0.5 ms a leaf (90 ms at 303 leaves, 480 ms at 1003, inside 1400 ms; past about 2000 leaves it would time out). A leaf with no validated key (one created after the swap, or a key dropped) is not left to the parent: the step adds the leaf's key `NOT VALID`, queues or re-opens its validation move, and re-opens itself (not a completion: no event, no retry counted); a leaf move that is failed or superseded fails the adoption loud, naming it, and a paused one blocks it with 55P03 naming the leaf. Gate 2 is unchanged: the chunks swap label is complete only while the composite is present and validated, and its reason now says it is waiting on the leaf validation moves and the adoption move (nexus-p2m1x.21).
- **Validations that block no writer have no statement bound, only a lock wait (Sam, 2026-11-23); they run before the claim, can be cancelled and are visible.** A move whose `statement_timeout_ms` is 2147483647 (`DataMove.NO_STATEMENT_BOUND_MS`; the column's CHECK refuses 0) runs its `on_complete` in a transaction of its own first, with `statement_timeout` 0 (so no network timeout either) and a 5 s `lock_timeout`, and then claims with the cheap re-check only (it is idempotent: it validates what is not validated yet). The status shows `validating for <time>` for it, the engine log names its backend (`event=data_move_completion`), stopping the engine cancels the statement, and a boot ends any `nexus-data-move-` backend a dead engine left before the migration runs. The registered stamp moves (their `on_complete` is `VALIDATE CONSTRAINT` of the P3.1 CHECKs) and the P3.3a id-twin validation moves that are not complete get that value from `vectors-081-1` (data only: one UPDATE of at most 23 registry rows, no DDL), and the swap registers its three child validation moves and the per-leaf ones with it. A step that blocks writers (the swap's `on_complete`, the adoption move) keeps its 1400 ms bound. The four boot-time admin validators keep their 30 minute bound. `Rdr229ValidationBoundsCensusIntegrationTest` pins every validation and constraint statement to its bound, and every file in the engine sources that holds one (nexus-p2m1x.21).
- **The chunks swap's verification no longer scans the chunks heap.** Its two READ ONLY warm-up scans (the anti-join of chunks to the registry and a count over a column no index holds) existed to warm the composite's inline validation; the composite is now added with no scan, so they warmed nothing and cost a full cold read under the 60 s verify bound that grows with the rows, where at three to five times today's size the first pass would have timed out on every retry. The verification is the leaf-key check alone (nexus-p2m1x.21).
