### Added

- **A data move that keeps failing now pages a person (RDR-246).** The worker still retries a transient failure by itself, backing off to 15 minutes. A move enters the needs-an-operator state at 20 consecutive transients, or at 3 consecutive lock (55P03) or statement (57014) timeouts at `on_complete`, because each such retry re-runs the warm-up and, on a chunk-family move, ends the in-flight index builds (PCI, leaf, id-unique). The early page is latched: once paged, another failure does not clear it and three more timeouts do not page again; it ends when the move completes or leaves the open set, or the row's retry count resets. Entering the state logs one ERROR, `event=data_move_needs_operator` (label, step, SQLSTATE, the bound missed, retries, `terminates_index_builds`), once per entry, and also for a move already in the state when the engine starts. `GET /v1/status` gains `data_moves_need_operator` (boolean), `data_moves_need_operator_labels` and `data_moves_need_operator_moves` (per move: retries, `last_attempt_at`, `next_retry_at`, `sqlstate`, `statement_timeout_ms`, `terminates_index_builds`, `step`, `bound_ms`, `retry_in_progress`). The flag clears when the move completes or its retry count resets. `nx doctor` shows such a move as a red row naming the label, the step and the bound it missed (a bound only for a lock or statement timeout), and the last attempt and the next retry; the retry-now hint warns when an attempt ends index builds; the `pg_background` warning is unchanged. The 3-in-a-row run and its latch are held by the engine process, so a restart starts it again (the 20-retries rule is read from the registry row and survives one).
