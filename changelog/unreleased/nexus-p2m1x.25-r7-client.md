### Changed

- **A request that names a collection retired with no successor gets one clear message.** The engine refuses, with a 409, a search, get, store get or list, topic or aspect read by the label of a collection that was retired and whose successor was deleted; it used to serve the old rows with 200. `nx search`, `nx store`, `nx collection info` and the other commands now say that the collection was retired and has no successor, so there is nothing under that label, and point at `nx collection list` and at indexing the files with `nx index md` or `nx index pdf` (`nx index repo` is refused while the repository's own collection is the retired one). They exit non-zero and do not retry. In a search over several collections the retired one is reported as failed and the others' hits are returned. `nx catalog show` prints its own marker for a link whose span sits in such a collection, and `nx collection re-embed` says the same when a plan or job names such a source or target.
- **Commands that walk every collection skip a retired one and say so.** A collection retired with no successor can still hold chunks, and the engine refuses a read of it by label. `nx store expire`, `nx store export --all`, `nx doctor --check-search`, `--check-embeddings` and `--check-assignments`, the PDF chunk metadata row, the per-collection `nx catalog doctor` checks, `nx enrich aspects backfill-uri`, the recovery-bundle export and the upgrade rungs that read chunk metadata now leave such a collection out, name it in their output (or log `retired_collection_skipped`), and do not report it as a failure. `nx store get` of one prints the retired-collection message instead of "not found" (nexus-p2m1x.25).
