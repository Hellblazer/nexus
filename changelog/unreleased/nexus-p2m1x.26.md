### Changed

- **A collection name is a label with its own rule, not a ChromaDB identifier (RDR-229 P3.6).** `validate_collection_name` and the engine's registration and rename routes accept any label that is non-empty, valid UTF-8, free of control characters (including U+2028 and U+2029), at most 255 UTF-8 bytes, and not of the form `c` followed by ASCII digits, which the engine reserves for the labels it mints. A 100-character label, one with a space, `%`, `:` or a non-ASCII letter, and one that does not start or end with a letter or digit are all accepted now; the 63-character cap, the 3-character minimum, the 128-byte cloud limit and the `nx catalog collection-name` overflow hint are gone. A label holding `/` is refused until the open label-alphabet question is ruled on. A NEW collection must still have a conformant name; the rule changes what a label may be, not what `nx` mints.
- **Engine: a label names one live collection per tenant.** `catalog-052-1` adds a partial UNIQUE index on `catalog_collections (tenant_id, name)` over the rows that are not superseded. The name is still the primary key until the P4.1 swap, so no existing row can fail it. A registration or rename with a refused label answers 400 and writes nothing.
