### Changed

- **No route accepts a `collection_id`.** The registration and ETL import routes answer 400 for a body that carries one; the tenant restore ignores the file's ids, reports the column skipped, and assigns each restored collection its id from the sequence. An import row, a restore file row or a named registration of a new collection on a live tuple another collection holds is refused naming both collections, with the live-tuple index present or not (without it the tuple is locked and re-read first), so none of them makes a second live row.
- **A registration that loses a race on the live-tuple index, and two renames onto one new name, are refused naming both collections (RDR-229 P3.8, engine half).** On the registration, import and rename routes a registration that loses a race on the live-tuple index is the same 409 naming both collections (it was an unnamed unique violation), and the re-embed start answers it as a 409. Two renames of different sources onto one new name now make one winner and a 409 for the other, which names both of its collections and leaves its source as it was (the loser used to retire its source naming an id no row holds and revive the winner's live row). A rename takes the locks of both tuples, its source's and its new row's, before it touches either row, when the index is absent, so it cannot wait on a registration that waits on it. A mint whose conflict target cannot be inferred onto an index of the live tuple's name, or whose insert meets that index's unique violation, takes the lock path or answers the winner's row, or a 409 telling it to try again, and is no longer a 500.
