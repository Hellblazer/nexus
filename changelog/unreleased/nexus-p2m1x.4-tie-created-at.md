### Changed

- **At an equal model version, `collectionForTuple` and the re-embed target selection now resolve a tuple to the collection registered first, not to the lexically lower name.** Two live collections on one tuple at the same version (a legacy slug repaired onto the documents' owner and its conformant sibling) used to resolve to the lower name. They now resolve to the older `created_at`, then to the name so the order stays total. `created_at` is first registration, except that a row backfilled by hygiene-001-6 carries its first document's `indexed_at` or the migration time, and a rename copies it onto the successor, so it is the age of the collection's lineage; equal stamps fall to the name. The flip is deliberate and plain: a legacy slug OLDER than its conformant sibling now wins the tie where the conformant name used to. The version is the same either way and the client re-renders the name from the owner, so `collection_for` is unaffected. This ships only after the RDR-229 P1.2 census finds no tuple where the flip changes the winner (Sam, 2026-11-20); P3.1's `collection_id` keeps the same rule. No wire or schema change (RDR-229 P1.4, nexus-p2m1x.4).
