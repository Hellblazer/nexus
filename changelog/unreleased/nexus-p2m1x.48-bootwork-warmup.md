### Fixed

- **`/v1/status` `boot_work` names a data move within a fraction of a second in the first minute after boot.** The data-move reporter sampled the registry once at start and then every 5 s, so a move registered after that first sample showed up to 5 s late: a first-run `nx doctor` read "no data move is open" and a capture a moment later listed the moves. The moves the engine registers at run time, after it serves, are the adoption move `rdr229-fkadopt-chunks`, the per-leaf validation moves, the per-table swap moves and the name-key drop move; a changeset registers the `rdr229-fkvalidate-*` moves at migrate, so those were never late. The reporter now samples every 250 ms for the first 60 s after it starts and every 5 s after that, so the steady-state sampling cost is unchanged; a sample that cannot read the registry is retried at 5 s. What `boot_work` reports is unchanged; only when. A move registered after the first minute still waits up to one 5 s period.
