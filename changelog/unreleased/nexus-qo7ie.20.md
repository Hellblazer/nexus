### Added

- **`nx memory curate --cold` lists cold tuple subspaces and writerless templates** (nexus-qo7ie.20, RDR-238 Phase 3 Step 3b, from RDR-239's dictionary view). A concrete subspace whose newest post (`newest_created_at`, consumed and expired rows included) is older than `--days` is listed with its last post, its counts and the owning template's `writers`; a template whose `writers` is empty or all marked `Unverified: ` (`board/<topic>` and `queue/<name>` today) and that has no subspace in the census is listed by name. The data comes from the same client calls as `nx tuple dictionary` (`registry(doc=True)` and the census), not from the CLI. An engine that serves no doc prints `not available: the engine predates the dictionary` and judges no template; the row does not depend on the transcripts, and the command still exits 0.
