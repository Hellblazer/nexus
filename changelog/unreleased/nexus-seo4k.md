### Changed

- **A lexical query of more than 8 words no longer runs the trigram half of the text gate** (nexus-seo4k; engine Liquibase `vectors-057` and `PgVectorRepository`, no client change, no wire change). The gate admitted a chunk when its full-text lexemes matched or its trigrams were similar, and the planner built the trigram index scan whatever the full-text half returned. For a sentence-length question that scan passed nothing on 35 of 35 measured questions and cost 20 to 30 times the full-text half. `nexus.text_gate_probe_<dim>` now holds both statements and chooses by the transaction-local setting `nexus.lexical_trgm`; the engine sets it to `off` for a gate text of more than 8 whitespace-separated words (`/hybrid-search` and the lexical leg of `--lexical`), and the probe then gates on the full-text half alone. Known loss: above 8 words a row that only the trigram half would pass is no longer returned: a pasted passage with an edit, an identifier or partial identifier inside a longer sentence, or a code window the english stemmer tokenises differently. At 8 words or fewer nothing changes. The 35 questions were a local check over 20,000 repository chunks; the real query-length distribution was not measured. The rank statements and the HNSW-first plan are unchanged, so a gate that passes the probe's cap still evaluates the trigram half per row there, and so does a request confined to a chunk set (`chash_allow`, at most 3000 rows), which never calls the probe.
