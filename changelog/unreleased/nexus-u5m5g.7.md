### Changed

- **`nx index repo` reconciles the catalog on the engine** (RDR-193 Step 6, nexus-u5m5g.7). The catalog hook of an index run walks the files, stats and hashes them and builds one landing row per file, then stages the rows on the engine in pages of at most 1000, commits the run once and polls the commit job; the diff against the catalog, the update of changed rows, the miss-count sweep, rename detection with its link moves and the explicit deletions of a delta run are the engine's, in one transaction. A run of N files is `ceil(N/1000)` stage requests and one commit where it was an owner list, a batch register, a batch update, a per-document housekeeping loop and a per-path delete. The job contract is the client's for every commit: a 409 waits for the named job and never resubmits (the run then raises, since its own rows were not committed), a 404 on the poll (the engine lost the job) resubmits once, a 503 from a full job registry or a failed job with reason `transient_db_error` resubmits at most three times with backoff, and any other failure raises and leaves the run's files unregistered, so no chunk is written under a document the catalog does not hold. There is no fallback to the old client path, which is deleted (`_run_housekeeping`, `_maybe_run_housekeeping`, `_delete_docs_for_paths`, `register_many`/`update_many` in the hook). Needs an engine that serves `/v1/catalog/reconcile/*` (engine-service-v0.1.185).
- **`nx store put`, `nx index md` and `nx index pdf` resolve a document with one `resolve_or_register` request** (RDR-193 Step 6). A titled note, and a curator-owned document with an identity of its own, are looked up by source URI (a ghost of the same title is reused for a note), and registered when absent, carrying the title, year and source mtime and born flagged when sensitive; the lookup, cross-owner probe, register and reconcile requests they made one after another are gone. A row another owner holds is reported and left as it is. The register-time stamp, author and corpus follow in one update. A document with a legacy relative path, a repository owner's row, or no absolute identity still takes the lookups it took before.
