### Added

- **Engine: `GET /v1/catalog/docs/by-scheme` reads the documents whose source URI has a given scheme (RDR-241 Phase 2).** `?scheme=<s>&limit=&offset=` answers `{count, limit, offset, documents}`: the live documents for which CPython 3.13's `urlparse(source_uri).scheme` equals the scheme lowercased, which is what the client's reference-only check keeps today, in the engine's text order of `tumbler` (so `5.1.12` sorts before `5.1.2`), a page of at most `limit` (1 to 1,000, default 1,000) at `offset`, with `count` the exact number of matches whatever the page. The statement mirrors `urlsplit`: leading controls and spaces (U+0001 to U+0020) are stripped, tab, CR and LF are removed from anywhere, and the scheme is the text before the first colon when it starts with an ASCII letter and holds only letters, digits and `+ - .`; so `HTTPS://x`, ` https://x` and `ht\ttps://x` are all `https`, and `C:\x` is `c`. `scheme` is required and must be such a scheme (a literal `+` in a query decodes to a space: send `%2B`), else 400; `limit` and `offset` out of range, a NUL byte or a malformed percent escape are a 400 too. A read that outlasts its statement bound answers 503 with `reason: statement_timeout`. `GET /version` gains `catalog_docs_by_scheme_supported: true`. The one place it differs from `urlparse` is a source URI that makes `urlsplit` raise (an invalid bracketed host): the client's call raises there, the route answers the scheme. No client calls it yet.
