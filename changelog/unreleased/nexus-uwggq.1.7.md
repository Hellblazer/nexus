### Changed

- **`nx catalog list --sensitive` reads the engine's sensitive list instead of scanning the catalog.** Without `--owner` it asks the engine (engine-service 0.1.188 or later) for the tenant's sensitive tumblers in tumbler order and resolves only what it needs: the first `--offset` + `-n` tumblers and just that page when there is no `--type` or `--meta` (so a first page works on a tenant of any size), every listed tumbler when there is. The rows, their order and the paging are the ones the scan gave. It never scans instead, and exits with a message when the engine does not advertise the route or answers 404, when the `GET /version` probe failed, when the route answers 503, fails or cannot be reached, or answers a body it cannot read, and when the list is capped (more than 10000 sensitive documents) and the command needs more than its first 10000: a `--type` or `--meta` listing, or a page reaching past the first 10000. That last message names `--owner` as the way to narrow. This is a change: the scan listed those cases, and on a tenant with more than 10000 sensitive documents a filtered or deep listing now refuses. RDR-241 Phase 2 closes it (a metadata route and a type filter on the sensitive route). `--owner` with `--sensitive` is unchanged. (nexus-uwggq.1.7, RDR-241)
