### Added

- **Engine: `GET /v1/catalog/docs/by-author` reads documents by author (RDR-241 Phase 2).** `?author=<text>&limit=&offset=` answers `{count, limit, offset, documents}`: the live documents whose author contains the text, case-insensitively, in the engine's text order of `tumbler` (the database's collation of a text column, so `5.1.12` sorts before `5.1.2`), a page of at most `limit` (1 to 1,000, default 1,000) at `offset`, with `count` the exact number of matches whatever the page. `author` is required and non-empty; `%`, `_` and `\` in it are literal; the match is `lower(author COLLATE "pg_c_utf8") LIKE '%' || lower(text COLLATE "pg_c_utf8") || '%'`: PostgreSQL 17's builtin collation with Unicode simple case mapping, so a local C-ctype cluster folds non-ASCII letters too, with no Unicode normalization on either side. It differs from Python's `str.lower()` (full mapping) only for U+0130 and for a capital sigma at the end of a word. A NUL byte or a malformed percent escape in a parameter is a 400. A read that outlasts its statement bound answers 503 with `reason: statement_timeout`. `GET /version` gains `catalog_docs_by_author_supported: true`. No client calls it yet.
