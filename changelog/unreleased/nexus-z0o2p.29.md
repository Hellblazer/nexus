### Fixed

- `write_many` with `chunks` no longer embeds or refreshes chunks that no registered document in the request lists, and the response reports how many it left out as `chunks_unreferenced`. Before, a chunk the collection already held was refreshed (its last-written time reset to now) even when no document of the request listed it.
- `append` and `append_many` refuse two rows at one position in one document, with a 400 naming the position (on `append_many` that document fails and the rest of the request is unaffected). Before, both chunks were inserted and only the second row was kept, leaving the first chunk with no owner.
- A chunk that loses its last manifest owner is now always recorded as orphaned, in the same transaction that dropped it. Before, a chunk written within the hour before the drop was skipped, so a re-index whose cleanup step timed out (or ran with the sweep off) could leave it with no owner and no orphan record. The reaper removes such a chunk after its grace period, counted from the drop; when an earlier record for the chunk is under an hour old it is left as it was, so the grace can start up to an hour before the drop. A chunk that is listed again by a later insert has its orphan record cleared. Chunks already left without an orphan record are not backfilled. The change is a schema migration (`vectors-061`) that redefines two triggers and adds one; it reaches users with the next engine release.
- Re-indexing a document no longer rewrites the rows of its manifest that did not change. The engine's manifest replace used to delete all of a document's rows and insert them back, which also wrote and cleared an orphan record for every chunk the document kept; it now deletes only the rows the new manifest lacks, updates the rows whose columns changed, and inserts the new ones. In a local test on a document of 5000 chunks, a re-index that changed nothing took about 0.03 s in the manifest write (it took about 1.4 s), and one that changed a tenth of the chunks about 0.1 s. A rewrite of more than 4000 rows of one document still takes the delete-and-insert path, at the same cost as before. The change is in the engine service and reaches users with the next engine release.
