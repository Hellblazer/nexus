### Fixed

- **A combined write no longer touches chunks that none of its documents list, and an append no longer accepts two rows at one position** (nexus-z0o2p.29). `write_many` with `chunks` used to refresh every chunk in the payload that the collection already held, including chunks that no document of the request listed, which reset their last-written time to now. A chunk refreshed that way and then dropped by a document within the hour was not recorded as orphaned, so it could stay in the collection with no document owning it. `write_many` now handles only chunks that the rows of a registered document reference, as `append` and `append_many` already did: an unlisted chunk is not embedded and is not refreshed, and the response reports how many were left out as `chunks_unreferenced`. Separately, `append` and `append_many` with two rows at the same position in one document used to insert both chunks and keep only the second row, leaving the first chunk in the collection with no owner. The engine now refuses such a request with a 400 naming the position (on `append_many`, that document fails and the rest of the request is unaffected) before any chunk is written. `write_many` already rejected a duplicate position and rolled back its chunks. The change is in the engine service and reaches users with the next engine release.
