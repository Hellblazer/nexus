### Changed

- **`nx_answer` lookups through the `hybrid-factual-lookup` plan rerank their retrieved chunks.** The plan's search step now asks for `lexical: true`, because MCP search applies the cross-encoder rerank only on that path. On 50 HERB "affected by" lookup questions (local engine) the share of gold companies reached in the top 20 went from 0.795 to 0.968 (paired +0.173, 95% CI +0.123 to +0.227; 27 of 50 questions improved, none worsened). The gain is the rerank: the lexical leg returned no rows on any of the 50 whole-question queries, and the rerank alone matched `lexical=true` on every question. Cost: the plan's search call goes from about 0.2 s to 5 to 10 s. An existing install picks the change up when the upgrade ladder reconciles the builtin plan library. Cloud-mode timing is checked before the release tag.
