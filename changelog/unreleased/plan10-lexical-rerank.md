### Changed

- **The `hybrid-factual-lookup` and `entity-resolution` plans rerank their search step's chunks.** Both plans' search step now asks for `lexical: true`, because MCP search applies the server cross-encoder rerank only on that path; `nexus-wohh7` adds a rerank parameter so the flag can go. Measured on a local engine, one HERB collection and the local cross-encoder, on 50 "affected by" lookup questions: the share of gold companies in the top 20 went from 0.795 to 0.968 (paired +0.173, 95% CI +0.123 to +0.227; 27 of 50 questions improved, none worsened). That is retrieval reach, not answer quality. The lexical leg returned no rows on any of the 50 whole-question queries and rerank alone matched `lexical=true` on every question (T2 `nexus/plan10-lexical-measure-2026-11-19`, `nexus_rdr/herb-full-pool-retrieval-variants-2026-11-19`). Cost: the search call goes from about 0.2 s to a p50 of about 5 s at limit 20 and about 10 s at limit 80 (p95 7 to 21 s), roughly 25 to 50% on a local plan run once its rank and generate steps are counted. Not measured: the plans' default limit of 40, other corpora or many collections at once, and cloud mode (there the rerank is Voyage's and uncapped over every fetched row). **Failure mode:** if the engine's hybrid-search leg errors (a service error, timeout or missing route; retried only on a statement timeout or fan-out budget stop), `search` returns an `Error:` string and the runner gives the plan an empty step-1 result with a warning, so the plan goes on with no evidence where before it had vector rows (`nexus-vi9u4`). An existing install picks the change up when the upgrade ladder reconciles the builtin plan library; a plan grown from a builtin keeps its old body.
