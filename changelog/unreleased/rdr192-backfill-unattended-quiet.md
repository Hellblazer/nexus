### Fixed

- **An unattended `nx upgrade` on a managed service skips the RDR-192 manifest backfill without a warning, and `nx doctor` and the version-change notice say once what to run.** The backfill never runs unattended on a managed service: its census is one engine scan per collection, and its data move belongs to an interactive `nx upgrade`. The rung declined only after the walk had picked it as pending, so every session start after a version bump logged a `ladder_rung_deferred` warning (608 on one install between 2026-10-09 and 2026-11-17).
  - The rung now declines in its check step, as the other data rungs do. The session-start upgrade skips it with no warning, and the rungs that must wait for it (section labels, the span fill, the retired-key sweep) still wait, as they did on a deferral. Any rung that an unattended check declines now holds its dependents the same way.
  - On a managed service, `nx doctor`'s "Upgrade ladder" row lists each rung it did not check with its reason, and its fix is an interactive `nx upgrade`. For the backfill the reason says either that a completion recorded at an earlier version still stands (the engine's reaper gate reads a record at any version) or that no completion is recorded, so the reaper stays refused.
  - On a managed service, the notice printed after a version change names a rung that an unattended check declined, once, with "run `nx upgrade` interactively". It used to name the backfill as pending with "run `nx upgrade`", which the session-start upgrade then declined.
  - On a local engine nothing changes: an unattended upgrade censuses and backfills within its time limit and resumes from its progress note.
