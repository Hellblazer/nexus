### Changed

- **The RDR-246 data-move finisher bounds its verification in two parts.** A new `nexus.data_moves.verify_statement_timeout_ms` column (default 60000 ms, `CHECK > 0`, changeset `datamoves-002-1`) is the statement timeout of the first, READ ONLY verification pass, which takes ACCESS SHARE only and is also the warm-up. The completion transaction keeps the move's `statement_timeout_ms` and its `lock_timeout`, and runs the verification again, warm, before it writes `completed_at`, per tenant as well as table-wide, over the first pass's tenants plus any the service tokens or partitions name now, so a write between the two is caught; a re-check that finds rows rolls the completion back and fails the move, and one that times out or waits out a lock rolls it back and retries. A first pass that times out is a verification failure under the existing retry and backoff. Every registered move takes the default; a registering INSERT needs no change.
