### Changed

- **Cloud installs keep seats (RDR-253 amendment, engine).** The data credential, the only one a cloud laptop holds, may now promote the ephemeral identity its own identity token names, create a delegated identity whose parent its token names, and create a seat when it carries a live token (the seat starts unheld, stamped `created_by identity:<id>`). Each call is fenced by the token: no token is 403 `identity_token_required`, another identity's is 403 `not_owner`, and a delegated child may not take a session another identity holds (409 `session_held`). Rename, `gone` and purge stay tenant-only, and the tenant credential is unchanged. `/version` carries `identity_self_seat_supported: true`. The engine adds changeset `identity-004` (the creator CHECK widened, no row changed).
