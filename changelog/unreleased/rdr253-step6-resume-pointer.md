### Added

- **After `/clear`, SessionStart prints one line saying where the session left off.** The PreCompact checkpoint hook, and a new SessionEnd hook for reason `clear`, keep a single entry tagged `resume-pointer` in the session's identity scratch (RDR-253 Step 6): the current thread (the one whose `worktree:` is the checkpoint's, else the one naming an in-progress bead, else the first), its worktree, `state:` and `waiting_on:`, at most 1 KB. The new entry is put first and the earlier pointers are deleted after it, so a failed put leaves the last one; no other identity-scratch entry is touched. When identity scratch cannot be written (no identity, a 401, a stalled engine, a spent budget) the hook logs one line with the error and writes nothing else. It is bounded, not free: PreCompact spends at most 6 s on it, only after the checkpoint file is written and never past 36 s into the hook, and SessionEnd at most 13 s. SessionStart reads it only for source `clear`, prints it as one line of at most 400 bytes with its age, drops a pointer older than 48 hours, and prints nothing when there is none or anything fails. Each `clear` start logs one `resume_line` event (`outcome` hit, empty, error or timeout, and `age_s`). The T1 guidance block gains one sentence: notes you want after `/clear` go to `scratch` with `scope=identity` and tag `working`, and session scope stays the layer subagents share.
- **The identity scratch scope is capped by the client.** `nx scratch --scope identity put` and the MCP `scratch` tool refuse an entry over 2 KB and refuse a non-pointer entry once the scope holds 20; the resume pointer has its own slot, and nothing is ever evicted. The engine enforces no cap, so this holds for clients that use `HttpScratchStore`; an engine-side cap can follow in a later engine release.
