### Added

- **`nx brief [TOPIC]` and the `brief` MCP tool.** Topics `memory`, `beads`, `caps`, `map`, `rdr` and `me`, each rendered by the same functions the hook used (the topic is case-insensitive); `nx brief` with no topic lists them, and `me` prints this seat's `startup` note (`identity/<id>`) as data, at most 2 KB. The MCP tool is read-only and auto-approved like the other read-only tools. Usage goes to `<config>/logs/brief.log` (rotated to `brief.log.1` past 1 MB): one `brief_load` line per call with the topic, the session id and the repo, and one `session_start` line per SessionStart as the denominator. The 30-day review: owner marginalia, due 30 days after the release that ships this change; retire a topic loaded in under 5% of sessions, and promote one loaded in over 60% back to the always-on block (RDR-253 Step 6, nexus-hczdo).
