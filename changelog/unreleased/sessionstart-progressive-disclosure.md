### Changed

- **SessionStart prints the rules and this session's state, and loads reference context on demand (`nx brief`).** On resume, clear and fork the always-on nexus output falls from about 5.6-5.8 KB (7-9 KB with a live T2 scan, the RDR line and the bd primer) to about 3.0-3.2 KB, 3.4-3.6 KB on clear with its resume line (RDR-253 Step 6, nexus-hczdo). The `session-context` hook now prints the T1 contract, the threads due, the identity-notes line, a warning when T2 is unreachable or stale (fire-only), `bd prime` for a project whose beads plugin is off, one index line (`More on demand (`nx brief <topic>` ...): beads ... before choosing work; memory, map before researching ...`) and the hygiene block. The T2 memory scan, the ready list, the capabilities block and the Knowledge Map are no longer injected, and a healthy RDR tree no longer prints its count line (the remedy, the unchecked-fix lines and the late-T2 note still print). What does not move behind a brief is what a session must see to work: the T2 reachability warning, the beads-off primer, the T1 contract, the due threads and the notes line. The full-prefix MCP rule in the capabilities block is carried by the project's own guidance (CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md), where it carries it, and by `nx brief caps`. The SessionStart byte ceilings in `tests/hooks/test_session_start_combined_budget.py` are lowered to the measured values, with an allowance per conditional line.
