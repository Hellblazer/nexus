### Changed

- The Stop wake guard catches a turn that ends on the session's own next step in the present tense, at the start of a clause ("I'm doing the switch myself", "Next I'm starting Step 5", "After that come the Trade-offs") with nothing armed, which it allowed before. A negation or an idiom ("I'm doing nothing", "I'm starting to think", "I'm doing well") and a relative or subordinate use ("anything I'm doing", "since I'm doing it") are not steps. A peer named only as the recipient of the session's own step ("handing the draft to halcyon") no longer reads as the wake, unless the clause carries a wait cue ("for wren once she's back"); a peer that reports, sends or gates still does. The continuation text adds the post-compaction checkpoint directive's own sentence, "Never start work the user held back or asked you to wait on, work that needs the user's approval, or work another session owns.", and a test keeps the two equal. On the 2026-11-12..19 transcripts the guard newly blocks 5 of 1955 unarmed turn ends on their full closing texts: the 3 known owned-work misses, one more owned step ("I'm fixing the follow-ups now"), and one plan-narrative line; none it blocked before is allowed now (T2 nexus/turn-end-owned-idle-census-2026-11-19).
